# 20 Questions to Ask on Your Adult Day Services Tour

*Adult day programs are often the first step into the care system — and the lifeline that keeps family caregivers going. Choose carefully.*

---

## Program Structure

1. **What are your hours of operation?** Is early drop-off or late pickup available?
2. **How many participants attend on a typical day?**
3. **What is the staff-to-participant ratio?**
4. **Is the program licensed or certified by the state?**
5. **What types of participants does your program serve best?** (Cognitive impairment, physical disability, social isolation, etc.)

## Care & Health Services

6. **What health monitoring is provided on-site?** (Blood pressure checks, medication administration, wound care)
7. **Is a nurse on site every day?** What medical conditions can you accommodate?
8. **How are medications handled during the day?**
9. **What is your protocol for a medical emergency?**
10. **Can participants with incontinence be accommodated?**

## Activities & Engagement

11. **What does a typical daily schedule look like?** Ask for a sample program calendar.
12. **What therapies are offered?** (Physical, occupational, speech, music, art)
13. **How are activities adapted for participants with different cognitive and physical abilities?**
14. **Are there social and recreational activities, or primarily structured therapy?**

## Transportation & Logistics

15. **Do you offer transportation to and from the program?** What is the coverage area and additional cost?
16. **What is the enrollment and intake process?**
17. **Is there a minimum number of days per week required?**

## Family Communication

18. **How do you keep family caregivers informed about how their loved one's day went?**
19. **How do you handle behavioral challenges** — a participant who becomes upset or disruptive?
20. **Can I spend a morning here to observe the program before enrolling?**

---

*A quality adult day program will welcome you to observe. That openness is itself a quality signal.*
