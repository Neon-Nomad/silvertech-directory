# 20 Questions to Ask on Your Assisted Living Tour

*Bring this guide on your visit. Take notes — impressions fade fast when comparing multiple communities.*

---

## Staffing & Care

1. **What is the staff-to-resident ratio** during day, evening, and overnight shifts?
2. **How are staff trained?** Is ongoing dementia and senior care training required?
3. **Who is responsible for medications?** Are they administered by licensed nurses or trained aides?
4. **What is your staff turnover rate** over the past 12 months?
5. **Is there a dedicated nurse on site** 24 hours a day, or on-call only?

## Daily Life & Culture

6. **What does a typical daily schedule look like?** Ask to see the activity calendar.
7. **Can residents personalize or furnish their own room?**
8. **What is the dining experience like?** Are there multiple meal options? Can residents request dietary accommodations?
9. **Are pets allowed?** What are the restrictions?
10. **What does your transportation program cover** — medical appointments, shopping, outings?

## Costs & Contracts

11. **What is included in the base monthly fee?** Get the full fee schedule in writing.
12. **What are the most common add-on charges?** (Medication management, incontinence care, extra laundry)
13. **How and how often can rates increase?** What notice is given?
14. **What is the move-out policy** if the community is not the right fit?

## Safety & Health

15. **How do you handle medical emergencies?** Is there a relationship with a nearby hospital?
16. **How is the building secured?** What prevents unauthorized entry or exit?
17. **How are care plans developed?** Who is involved, and how often are they reviewed?
18. **What happens if a resident's care needs increase significantly?** Do you have memory care on-site?

## Transparency

19. **Have there been any state licensing violations or citations** in the past two years? Can I see the inspection report?
20. **Can I speak with current residents or their family members** as references before I decide?

---

*Note the name of the person who gave you the tour. Their responsiveness today is a preview of how the community communicates with families.*
