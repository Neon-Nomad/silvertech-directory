# 20 Questions to Ask on Your CCRC Tour

*A Continuing Care Retirement Community (CCRC) is one of the largest financial and lifestyle decisions a person can make. These questions protect you.*

---

## Contract & Financial Structure

1. **What type of contract is offered?** Type A (all-inclusive), Type B (modified), or Type C (fee-for-service)?
2. **What is the entrance fee**, and how much is refundable — and under what conditions?
3. **What does the monthly fee include?** What triggers additional charges?
4. **How often and by how much have monthly fees increased over the past five years?**
5. **What happens if I outlive my financial resources?** Is there a benevolence fund or Medicaid acceptance?

## Financial Health of the Organization

6. **Can I see the community's most recent audited financial statements?**
7. **What is the current occupancy rate across all levels of care?**
8. **Is this community part of a larger nonprofit or for-profit organization?**
9. **Has the community ever been in financial distress, changed ownership, or been acquired?**
10. **Is the community accredited by CARF or another body?**

## Continuum of Care

11. **What levels of care are available on this campus?** (Independent, assisted, memory care, skilled nursing)
12. **Is priority access to higher levels of care guaranteed in my contract?**
13. **What triggers a move from one level of care to another, and who makes that decision?**
14. **Can I return to independent living after a skilled nursing stay?**
15. **Are hospice services available on-site?**

## Daily Life & Community

16. **What does the independent living lifestyle look like?** Activities, dining, transportation?
17. **How are residents involved in governance and community decisions?**
18. **Can I bring my pet?**
19. **What is the visitor and guest policy?** Can family members stay?

## Transparency

20. **Can I speak with a current resident and a family member of someone in the care center?**

---

*Have an independent financial advisor and an elder law attorney review the contract before signing. This is not optional.*
