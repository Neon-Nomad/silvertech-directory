# 20 Questions to Ask on Your Residential Care Home Tour

*Residential care homes — also called board and care homes — are small (typically 6 to 10 residents). The intimate setting is the appeal. These questions help you make sure the reality matches the promise.*

---

## Size, Staffing & Licensing

1. **How many residents currently live here**, and what is the maximum licensed capacity?
2. **How many staff are on duty during the day, evening, and overnight?**
3. **Is this home licensed by the state?** Can I see the current license and most recent inspection?
4. **How long has the owner/operator been running this home?**
5. **Who provides care if the primary caregiver is sick or on vacation?**

## Care Capability

6. **What level of personal care can you provide?** (Bathing, dressing, toileting, transfers)
7. **How are medications managed and administered?** Are staff certified to handle medications?
8. **What medical conditions are you experienced with?** (Parkinson's, diabetes, post-surgical recovery)
9. **What conditions or care needs are beyond what you can handle?**
10. **Do you accept residents on hospice?**

## Daily Life

11. **What does a typical day look like for residents?**
12. **What meals are provided, and can dietary restrictions be accommodated?**
13. **Is the home smoke-free? Are pets allowed?**
14. **What activities or outings are available?**
15. **Can residents bring their own furniture and belongings?**

## Costs & Agreements

16. **What is included in the monthly fee?** Get the full list in writing.
17. **What are the most common additional charges?**
18. **What is the move-out or discharge policy** if care needs exceed what you can provide?
19. **What happens to the monthly fee if a resident is hospitalized temporarily?**

## References & Transparency

20. **Can I speak with a current resident's family member** as a reference before I decide?

---

*In a small home, the operator's character matters more than in a large facility. Trust your gut about the person running it.*
