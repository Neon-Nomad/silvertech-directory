# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Colorado

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, including Medicaid, licensing, and advocacy, requires direct access to the correct state and federal agencies. This document provides a consolidated, verified list of essential contact information for the primary governmental and non-profit authorities responsible for regulating, funding, and advocating for long-term care services in Colorado.

This information is crucial for residents, family members, caregivers, and professionals seeking to apply for benefits, file a complaint, or obtain regulatory information regarding nursing homes and assisted living residences in the state.

## I. Medicaid and Financial Assistance Authority

The state's Medicaid program, **Health First Colorado**, is the primary source of funding for long-term care services for eligible residents. The program is administered by the Department of Health Care Policy and Financing (HCPF).

| Agency/Program | Primary Function | Phone Number | Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Health First Colorado (Colorado Medicaid)** | General information, eligibility, and benefits for Medicaid. | (800) 221-3943 (State Relay: 711) | https://hcpf.colorado.gov/ |
| **Department of Health Care Policy and Financing (HCPF)** | State agency administering Health First Colorado and the Elderly, Blind, and Disabled (EBD) Waiver. | (303) 866-2993 (General) | https://hcpf.colorado.gov/ |
| **PEAK (Program Eligibility and Application Kit)** | Online portal for applying for Health First Colorado and other state benefits. | (800) 221-3943 | https://www.colorado.gov/peak/ |

## II. Licensing, Regulation, and Complaint Authority

The **Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment (CDPHE)** is the state's regulatory body responsible for licensing and certifying health facilities, including nursing homes and assisted living residences.

| **CDPHE - Health Facilities and Emergency Medical Services Division** | Licensing, certification, and regulation of nursing homes and assisted living facilities. | (303) 692-2442 | https://cdphe.colorado.gov/nursing-homes |
| **CDPHE - Health Facilities Complaint Hotline** | Dedicated line for reporting complaints against licensed health facilities (nursing homes, assisted living, etc.). | (800) 886-7689 ext. 2442 | https://cdphe.colorado.gov/health-facilities-complaints |
| **CDPHE - Complaint Email Intake** | Email address for submitting written complaints. | N/A | cdphe.hfdintake@state.co.us |
