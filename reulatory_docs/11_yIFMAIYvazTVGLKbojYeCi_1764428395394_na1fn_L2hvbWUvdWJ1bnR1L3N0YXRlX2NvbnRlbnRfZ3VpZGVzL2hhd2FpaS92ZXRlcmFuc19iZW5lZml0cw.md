# Hawaii Veterans Benefits: A Comprehensive Guide to State Support and Long-Term Care

The State of Hawaii, with its deep connection to the U.S. military and a significant veteran population, offers a comprehensive suite of benefits and services to honor those who have served. These benefits, administered primarily through the **Office of Veterans' Services (OVS)**, a division of the Department of Defense, are designed to supplement federal benefits and provide critical support in areas such as long-term care, financial relief, and employment [1].

This guide provides a detailed overview of the key veterans benefits available in Hawaii, with a special focus on the state's two Veterans Homes and the financial and employment advantages for service members and their families.

## State Veterans Homes: Long-Term Care in Hawaii

Hawaii operates two State Veterans Homes, which provide high-quality, long-term care, including skilled nursing and rehabilitation services, to eligible veterans, their spouses, and Gold Star parents. These homes are a critical component of the state's long-term care continuum, offering a dignified and specialized environment for those who require a higher level of medical care.

| Veterans Home | Location | Capacity/Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yukio Okutsu State Veterans Home** | Hilo, Hawaii (Big Island) | Provides skilled nursing and residential long-term placement. |
| **Daniel Kahikina Akaka State Veterans Home** | Kapolei, Oahu | A state-of-the-art, 120-bed facility focusing on post-acute care and rehabilitation services. |

### Services Provided

The State Veterans Homes offer a range of services comparable to private skilled nursing facilities, but with a focus on the unique needs of the veteran population. These services typically include:

*   **24-Hour Skilled Nursing Care:** Comprehensive medical care provided by licensed nurses.
*   **Rehabilitation Services:** Physical, occupational, and speech therapy.
*   **Long-Term Residential Care:** A permanent home for veterans requiring ongoing assistance.
*   **Hospice and Palliative Care:** Specialized care for end-of-life comfort.
*   **Dementia and Alzheimer's Care:** Dedicated units and programs for residents with cognitive impairment.
*   **Social and Recreational Activities:** Programs designed to promote camaraderie and quality of life.

### Eligibility and Cost of Care

Admission to a Hawaii State Veterans Home requires meeting specific criteria, which typically include:

1.  **Veteran Status:** Must be a recognized veteran of the U.S. Armed Forces with a discharge other than dishonorable (DD-214 required) [2].
2.  **Residency:** Must be a resident of the State of Hawaii.
3.  **Medical Need:** Must require skilled nursing or intermediate level of care, as determined by a medical assessment.
4.  **Spouses and Parents:** Spouses of eligible veterans and Gold Star parents (parents of a service member who died while serving) may also be eligible for admission on a space-available basis.

**Cost of Care:** The cost of care is determined on a sliding scale based on the veteran's income and assets. However, a significant benefit is that the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) provides a per diem payment to the state for eligible veterans. Furthermore, veterans with a **70% or higher service-connected disability rating** often have their cost of care fully covered by the VA [3].

## Financial and Tax Benefits for Hawaii Veterans

Hawaii offers several significant financial benefits that can provide substantial relief to veterans and their families, particularly those with service-connected disabilities.

### Property Tax Exemptions

One of the most valuable benefits is the **property tax exemption** for disabled veterans.

*   **Total Exemption:** A veteran who is certified by the VA as being **100% totally disabled** due to service-connected injuries is eligible for a **full exemption** from all property taxes on their primary residence [4]. This exemption is a major financial relief and is administered at the county level (Honolulu, Maui, Hawaii, and Kauai).
*   **Partial Exemptions:** Veterans with lower disability ratings or those who do not meet the 100% threshold may still qualify for other property tax relief programs offered by their respective counties.

### Income Tax Exemptions

Hawaii provides favorable treatment for military-related income:

*   **Military Retirement Pay:** All military retirement pay and Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP) payments are **fully exempt** from Hawaii state income tax [5]. This is a significant advantage for retired service members residing in the state.
*   **Active Duty Pay:** Active-duty military pay is subject to federal tax rules, but Hawaii offers a partial exemption for certain types of military income, such as combat pay.

### Vehicle and Licensing Benefits

*   **Veteran Designation on Driver's License:** Veterans can have a "VETERAN" designation added to their Hawaii driver's license or state identification card, which can be used to prove veteran status for discounts and services.
*   **Disabled Veteran License Plates:** Veterans with a service-connected disability can apply for special disabled veteran license plates, which may offer certain parking privileges.

## Employment and Education Benefits

The State of Hawaii recognizes the value of military service by providing advantages in public employment and education.

### Veterans' Preference in State Employment

Veterans are given a preference when applying for State of Hawaii civil service jobs. This preference is applied to the scoring process for examinations:

*   **5-Point Preference:** Honorably discharged veterans are eligible to have **five points** added to their final passing score on civil service examinations [6].
*   **10-Point Preference:** Veterans with a service-connected disability of **30% or more** are eligible for a **ten-point preference** [6].

### Employment Services

The Hawaii Department of Labor and Industrial Relations (DLIR), through its Workforce Development Division (WDD), offers specialized employment services for veterans. These services include:

*   **Priority of Service:** Veterans receive priority access to job referrals, training programs, and other services at all American Job Centers.
*   **Specialized Staff:** Disabled Veterans' Outreach Program (DVOP) specialists and Local Veterans' Employment Representatives (LVERs) are available to provide intensive case management, job development, and placement services.

### Education Benefits

While the federal Post-9/11 GI Bill is the primary education benefit, Hawaii offers supplemental support:

*   **Hawaii National Guard Tuition Waiver:** Members of the Hawaii National Guard may be eligible for a tuition waiver at the University of Hawaii system institutions.
*   **Residency for Tuition:** Veterans and their dependents may be eligible for in-state tuition rates at public colleges and universities immediately upon establishing residency in Hawaii, regardless of the standard 12-month waiting period.

## The Office of Veterans' Services (OVS)

The **Office of Veterans' Services (OVS)** is the central resource for all veterans in Hawaii. OVS serves as the principal state office responsible for assisting veterans, their dependents, and survivors in obtaining state and federal entitlements.

*   **Advocacy and Claims Assistance:** OVS staff are trained to assist veterans with filing VA claims, completing necessary forms, and appealing adverse decisions.
*   **Information and Referral:** The office provides up-to-date information on all available benefits and connects veterans with the appropriate service providers.
*   **Outreach:** OVS conducts outreach programs across all islands to ensure veterans in rural and remote areas are aware of and can access their benefits.

## Conclusion

Hawaii’s commitment to its veterans is evident in its robust system of support, from the state-of-the-art Veterans Homes providing essential long-term care to the significant financial and employment advantages. By leveraging the resources of the Office of Veterans' Services, Hawaii veterans can successfully navigate the complex landscape of benefits and ensure they receive the full range of entitlements they have earned through their service.
