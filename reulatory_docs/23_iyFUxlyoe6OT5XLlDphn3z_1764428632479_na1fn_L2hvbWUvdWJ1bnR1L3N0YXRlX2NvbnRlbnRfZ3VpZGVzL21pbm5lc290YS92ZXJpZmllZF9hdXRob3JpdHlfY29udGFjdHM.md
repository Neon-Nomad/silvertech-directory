## VII. Utilizing the Contacts Effectively

To ensure a smooth and effective interaction with these state authorities, follow these best practices:

1.  **Know the Agency’s Role:** Before calling, confirm which agency is responsible for your specific issue (e.g., DHS for funding, MDH for licensing, MAARC for abuse reports).
2.  **Prepare Information:** Have all relevant details ready, including names, dates, facility addresses, and a clear, concise description of the issue.
3.  **Document Everything:** Keep a detailed log of every contact, including the date, time, the name of the person you spoke with, and a summary of the conversation.
4.  **Start with the Ombudsman for Guidance:** If you are unsure where to direct your concern, the OOLTC is an excellent, confidential starting point that can triage your issue to the correct authority.
