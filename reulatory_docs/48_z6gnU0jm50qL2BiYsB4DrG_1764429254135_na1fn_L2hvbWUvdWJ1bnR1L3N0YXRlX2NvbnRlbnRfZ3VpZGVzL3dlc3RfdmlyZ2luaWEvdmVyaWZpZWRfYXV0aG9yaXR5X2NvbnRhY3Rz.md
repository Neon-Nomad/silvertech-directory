# West Virginia Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Long-Term Care

Navigating the long-term care system in West Virginia requires access to accurate and reliable information from state and federal authorities. This guide compiles a comprehensive list of verified contact information for the key governmental and non-profit agencies responsible for regulating, funding, and advocating for long-term care services in the state. These contacts are essential resources for residents, family members, caregivers, and healthcare professionals seeking assistance, filing complaints, or inquiring about eligibility for services.

## I. Medicaid and Financial Assistance

The **Bureau for Medical Services (BMS)** is the state agency that administers the West Virginia Medicaid Program, including the critical Aged and Disabled Waiver (ADW) for long-term care.

| Agency | Program/Function | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bureau for Medical Services (BMS)** | State Medicaid Agency, Policy, and Administration | [https://bms.wv.gov/](https://bms.wv.gov/) | (304) 558-1700 |
| **WV ASO - Acentra Health** | Aged & Disabled Waiver (ADW) Assessment and Management | [https://wvaso.acentra.com/](https://wvaso.acentra.com/) | (866) 385-8920 (Toll-Free) |
| **WV Department of Health and Human Resources (DHHR)** | Application for Medicaid and other Family Assistance Benefits | [https://dhhr.wv.gov/](https://dhhr.wv.gov/) | (304) 558-0684 (General DHHR) |

**Key Focus:** Eligibility for the **West Virginia Medicaid Program** and the **Aged and Disabled Waiver (ADW)**, which provides home and community-based services as an alternative to nursing home placement, including coverage for services in assisted living residences.

## II. Licensing, Certification, and Complaint Investigation

The **Office of Health Facility Licensure and Certification (OHFLAC)** is the regulatory body responsible for ensuring the quality and safety of long-term care facilities through licensing, certification, and complaint investigation.

| Agency | Program/Function | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OHFLAC** | Licensing and Certification of Nursing Homes and Assisted Living | [https://ohflac.wvdhhr.org/](https://ohflac.wvdhhr.org/) | (304) 558-0050 |
| **OHFLAC Complaint Hotline** | Reporting complaints against licensed healthcare facilities | [https://ohflac.wvdhhr.org/complaint.html](https://ohflac.wvdhhr.org/complaint.html) | (304) 558-0050 |
| **Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)** | Federal oversight of Medicare/Medicaid certified facilities | [https://www.cms.gov/](https://www.cms.gov/) | (800) 633-4227 (Medicare General) |

**Key Focus:** Filing formal complaints regarding quality of care, facility conditions, or resident rights violations. OHFLAC conducts unannounced surveys and investigations to enforce state and federal regulations.

## III. Resident Advocacy and Ombudsman Services

The **Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** provides confidential advocacy and complaint resolution services for residents of long-term care facilities, ensuring their rights are protected.

| Agency | Program/Function | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WV Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** | Resident advocacy, rights education, and complaint resolution | [https://legalaidwv.org/our-programs/long-term-care/](https://legalaidwv.org/our-programs/long-term-care/) | (800) 834-0598 (Toll-Free) |
| **Legal Aid of West Virginia, Inc.** | Administering agency for the Ombudsman Program | [https://legalaidwv.org/](https://legalaidwv.org/) | (304) 343-4481 (Charleston Main Office) |
| **The Consumer Voice** | National resource for long-term care quality and advocacy | [https://theconsumervoice.org/](https://theconsumervoice.org/) | (202) 332-2275 |

**Key Focus:** Confidential assistance with resident rights issues, quality of life concerns, and mediation between residents/families and facility staff.

## IV. Veterans' Benefits and Services

The **West Virginia Department of Veterans Assistance (WVDVA)** is the primary state agency assisting veterans and their families in accessing state and federal benefits, including long-term care options.

| Agency | Program/Function | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WV Department of Veterans Assistance (WVDVA)** | State veterans' benefits, claims assistance, and advocacy | [https://veterans.wv.gov/](https://veterans.wv.gov/) | (304) 558-3661 |
| **U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)** | Federal VA benefits, including Aid and Attendance Pension | [https://www.va.gov/](https://www.va.gov/) | (800) 827-1000 (VA Benefits Hotline) |
| **VA Health Care System** | VA long-term care services and facilities | [https://www.va.gov/health-care/](https://www.va.gov/health-care/) | (877) 222-8387 (VA Health Care Eligibility) |

**Key Focus:** Information on the **State Veterans Nursing Facility**, assistance with filing for the **VA Aid and Attendance Pension**, and general benefits counseling.

## V. Elder Abuse and Protective Services

Reporting suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of vulnerable adults is a legal and moral imperative. **Adult Protective Services (APS)** is the state agency responsible for investigating these reports.

| Agency | Program/Function | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adult Protective Services (APS)** | Investigation of abuse, neglect, and exploitation of vulnerable adults | [https://dhhr.wv.gov/bcf/Services/aps/Pages/default.aspx](https://dhhr.wv.gov/bcf/Services/aps/Pages/default.aspx) | (800) 352-6513 (APS Hotline) |
| **West Virginia State Police** | Law enforcement for criminal matters | [https://wvsp.gov/](https://wvsp.gov/) | 911 (Emergency) or Local Detachment |

**Key Focus:** Confidential reporting of suspected harm to an elderly or disabled adult in any setting, including long-term care facilities.

## VI. Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC)

The **Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC)**, often managed by local Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs), serves as a single point of entry for information and access to long-term care services and supports.

| Agency | Program/Function | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **WV Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC)** | Information and referral for long-term services and supports | [https://www.wvseniorservices.gov/](https://www.wvseniorservices.gov/) | (866) 988-4460 (Toll-Free) |
| **Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs)** | Local services, senior centers, and community programs | [https://www.wvseniorservices.gov/agencies/](https://www.wvseniorservices.gov/agencies/) | Varies by region |

**Key Focus:** Comprehensive, unbiased information on all long-term care options, including in-home care, assisted living, and nursing homes, as well as public and private funding sources.

## Conclusion: Empowering the Consumer

This compilation of verified authority contacts serves as a critical resource for anyone involved in the long-term care journey in West Virginia. Whether seeking financial assistance through the **Bureau for Medical Services**, reporting a safety concern to **OHFLAC**, advocating for a resident's rights with the **Ombudsman Program**, or accessing earned benefits through the **WVDVA**, having these verified contacts readily available is the first step toward ensuring quality care and accountability. Consumers are encouraged to save this list and utilize these resources to make informed decisions and secure the best possible outcomes for themselves or their loved ones.
