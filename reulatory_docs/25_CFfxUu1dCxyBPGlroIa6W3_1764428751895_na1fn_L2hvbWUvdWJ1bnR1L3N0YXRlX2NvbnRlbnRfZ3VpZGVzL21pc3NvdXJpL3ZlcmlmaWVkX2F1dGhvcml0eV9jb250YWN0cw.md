# Missouri Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Long-Term Care

## Introduction to Missouri's Long-Term Care Authority Network

Navigating the long-term care system in Missouri requires interaction with several key state agencies, each responsible for a distinct aspect of care, funding, regulation, and advocacy. This guide provides a comprehensive, verified directory of the most critical governmental and state-mandated non-profit authorities that seniors, individuals with disabilities, and their families will need to contact for information, applications, complaints, and advocacy.

The information provided here is current and verified, serving as the essential starting point for accessing services related to MO HealthNet (Medicaid), facility licensing, resident rights, and veterans' benefits.

## 1. Medicaid Programs: MO HealthNet Division

The **MO HealthNet Division** of the Missouri Department of Social Services (DSS) is the single state agency responsible for administering the state's Medicaid program. This agency manages eligibility determination, enrollment, and the payment of claims for long-term care services, including Nursing Home Medicaid, the Supplemental Nursing Care (SNC) program, and the financial aspects of Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) Waivers.

### Missouri Department of Social Services, MO HealthNet Division

| Contact Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Name** | Missouri Department of Social Services, MO HealthNet Division [1] |
| **Primary Function** | Administers the MO HealthNet (Medicaid) program, determines financial eligibility for long-term care, and manages the Supplemental Nursing Care (SNC) program. |
| **Website** | [https://mydss.mo.gov/healthcare]() |
| **General Information Phone** | **(800) 348-6627** |
| **Mailing Address** | MO HealthNet Division, P.O. Box 6500, Jefferson City, MO 65102-6500 |
| **Application Submission** | Applications for MO HealthNet can be submitted online or at a local Family Support Division (FSD) office. |

### Key Services Provided by MO HealthNet:
*   **Eligibility Screening:** Determining financial eligibility for all MO HealthNet programs, including the Aged, Blind, and Disabled (ABD) category.
*   **Long-Term Care Funding:** Managing the financial contribution for nursing home care and the cash assistance for assisted living (SNC).
*   **Consumer-Directed Services (CDS):** Overseeing the financial and administrative aspects of the CDS model for personal care.

## 2. Licensing, Regulation, and Public Safety: DHSS

The **Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services (DHSS)** is the state's regulatory authority for all long-term care facilities. The DHSS, through its **Section for Long-Term Care Regulation (SLTCR)**, is responsible for licensing facilities, conducting unannounced inspections, investigating complaints related to quality of care, and ensuring compliance with state and federal standards.

### Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services, Section for Long-Term Care Regulation

| Contact Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Name** | Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services, Section for Long-Term Care Regulation [2] |
| **Primary Function** | Licenses and inspects all long-term care facilities (Nursing Homes, ALFs, RCFs), conducts federal certification surveys, and investigates quality of care complaints. |
| **Website** | [https://health.mo.gov/seniors/nursinghomes/licensecert.php]() |
| **Licensing Phone** | **(573) 751-6400** |
| **Mailing Address** | Section for Long Term Care Regulation, P.O. Box 570, Jefferson City, MO 65102-0570 |
| **Public Inspection Reports** | **Show Me Long-Term Care** website: [https://www.dhss.mo.gov/safety/showmelongtermcare/]() |

### Key Services Provided by DHSS/SLTCR:
*   **Facility Licensing:** Issuing and renewing licenses for all Residential Care, Assisted Living, and Skilled Nursing Facilities.
*   **Inspections:** Conducting mandatory, unannounced inspections (twice per fiscal year for state-licensed facilities).
*   **Complaint Investigation:** Investigating allegations of regulatory non-compliance and quality of care issues.

## 3. Ombudsman and Advocacy: Resident Rights Protection

The **Missouri Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** is a state-mandated advocacy service that works to protect the rights and welfare of residents in long-term care facilities. It is a confidential resource for residents and families to resolve complaints and advocate for systemic change.

### Missouri Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

| Contact Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Program Name** | Missouri Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program [3] |
| **Administering Agency** | Missouri Department of Health and Senior Services, Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman |
| **Primary Function** | Investigates and resolves complaints made by or on behalf of residents, advocates for resident rights, and provides information on long-term care issues. |
| **Website** | [https://health.mo.gov/seniors/ombudsman/]() |
| **Toll-Free Phone** | **(800) 309-3282** |
| **Mailing Address** | Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman, P.O. Box 570, Jefferson City, MO 65102-0570 |

### Key Services Provided by the Ombudsman:
*   **Complaint Resolution:** Mediating disputes between residents, families, and facility staff/management.
*   **Confidential Advocacy:** Providing a confidential channel for residents to voice concerns without fear of retaliation.
*   **Volunteer Program:** Recruiting and training volunteer ombudsmen to make regular visits to facilities.

## 4. Abuse and Neglect Reporting: The Central Hotline

The most critical contact for reporting suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of an elderly person or an adult with a disability is the centralized, 24/7 hotline. This single point of contact ensures that all serious allegations are immediately triaged and assigned to the appropriate investigative unit (DHSS, DSS Adult Protective Services, or the Office of Special Investigations).

### Missouri Adult Abuse and Neglect Hotline

| Contact Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hotline Name** | Missouri Adult Abuse and Neglect Hotline [4] |
| **Primary Function** | Receives and triages all reports of suspected abuse, neglect, exploitation, or isolation of vulnerable adults. |
| **Toll-Free Phone** | **(800) 392-0210** |
| **Online Reporting** | [https://moapss.health.mo.gov/]() |
| **Availability** | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. |

### Key Services Provided by the Hotline:
*   **Immediate Triage:** Rapidly assessing the severity of the report and assigning it to the correct investigative agency.
*   **Confidential Reporting:** Protecting the identity of the reporter, which is crucial for mandatory reporters and concerned family members.
*   **Emergency Response:** Initiating an immediate investigation for life-threatening or severe allegations.

## 5. Veterans Benefits and Services: Missouri Veterans Commission

The **Missouri Veterans Commission (MVC)** is the state agency dedicated to assisting veterans and their families in securing state and federal benefits, including long-term care options such as the State Veterans Homes and the VA Aid and Attendance Pension.

### Missouri Veterans Commission

| Contact Category | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Name** | Missouri Veterans Commission [5] |
| **Primary Function** | Manages the seven State Veterans Homes, provides Veterans Service Officers (VSOs) for claims assistance, and administers state veterans' benefits. |
| **Website** | [https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/]() |
| **General Phone** | **(573) 751-3779** |
| **Mailing Address** | Missouri Veterans Commission, P.O. Box 147, Jefferson City, MO 65102-0147 |
| **Veterans Homes Information** | [https://mvc.dps.mo.gov/homes/]() |

### Key Services Provided by the MVC:
*   **State Veterans Homes:** Providing skilled nursing care to eligible veterans and their spouses at seven locations.
*   **VSO Assistance:** Offering free, accredited assistance with filing claims for VA benefits, including disability compensation and the Aid and Attendance Pension.
*   **State Benefits:** Administering state-specific benefits such as property tax credits and educational assistance.

## Conclusion: Empowering Access to Care

The Missouri long-term care landscape is supported by a network of dedicated state authorities. By consolidating the contact information for the MO HealthNet Division, the Licensing Authority (DHSS/SLTCR), the Ombudsman Program, the Abuse and Neglect Hotline, and the Missouri Veterans Commission, this guide serves as an indispensable resource. Empowering residents and their families with these verified contacts ensures that they can efficiently access the necessary funding, regulatory oversight, and advocacy services to secure the highest quality of care.
