# Verified Authority Contacts for Senior and Long-Term Care in Rhode Island

Navigating the complex landscape of senior services, long-term care, and veterans benefits in Rhode Island requires access to accurate and reliable information. This comprehensive guide provides a verified directory of the most critical state and federal agencies, advocacy organizations, and hotlines dedicated to serving the elderly, individuals with disabilities, and veterans in the Ocean State.

These contacts are the authoritative sources for information on Medicaid eligibility, facility licensing, complaint reporting, and accessing earned benefits. Utilizing these official channels ensures that residents and their families receive the most current and verified guidance.

## I. Health and Human Services: Medicaid and Long-Term Care

## VI. Utilizing the Contact Network for Maximum Support

Understanding the distinct roles of these agencies is crucial for effective problem-solving.

### The Regulatory vs. Advocacy Distinction

It is important to distinguish between the **regulatory** function of the RIDOH and the **advocacy** function of the Ombudsman.
*   **RIDOH** acts as the state's police force for healthcare facilities. Their investigations are formal, focus on regulatory compliance, and can result in fines or license revocation. They are the correct contact for issues of substandard care or safety violations.
*   **The Ombudsman** acts as a mediator and advocate. They are confidential, non-regulatory, and often resolve issues more quickly through negotiation. They are the best first contact for quality of life issues, resident rights concerns, and grievances that do not immediately threaten health or safety.

### The Importance of the ADRC (The POINT)

The **Aging and Disability Resource Center (The POINT)** is designed to simplify the initial search for services. Instead of calling multiple agencies, a single call to The POINT can connect an individual to a specialist who can assess their needs and provide referrals to the correct state and community programs, including Medicaid application assistance, home-delivered meals, and transportation services. This service is particularly valuable for family caregivers who are overwhelmed by the complexity of the system.

### Protecting Veterans' Long-Term Care Needs

Veterans in Rhode Island have a unique set of resources. The **Rhode Island Veterans Home** provides a dedicated long-term care option, and RIVETS is the essential contact for maximizing all state and federal benefits, which can significantly offset the cost of long-term care, including the **Disabled Veterans Tuition Waiver** and property tax exemptions. Veterans should always contact RIVETS before making major decisions about their benefits or care.

## Conclusion

Rhode Island has established a comprehensive and accessible network of verified authority contacts to support its senior, disabled, and veteran populations. By knowing which agency to contact for a specific need—from the **RIDOH's Complaint Hotline** for facility violations to the **APS Hotline** for elder abuse, and the **Ombudsman** for rights advocacy—residents and their families can effectively navigate the system. This directory serves as the essential starting point for accessing the high-quality care, benefits, and support services that Rhode Island is committed to providing.
