# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Texas

**State:** Texas
**Key Authority:** Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC)
**Centralized Information Portal:** Texas Unified Licensure Information Portal (TULIP)
**Purpose:** A comprehensive directory of essential state and federal agencies, programs, and advocacy groups that govern, regulate, and support long-term care services in Texas.

## Introduction to Texas Long-Term Care Authorities

Navigating the long-term care landscape in Texas requires interacting with several key governmental and non-profit organizations. These entities are responsible for everything from licensing facilities and administering Medicaid to advocating for resident rights and providing veterans' benefits. This document serves as a verified, comprehensive directory of the most critical contacts for consumers, family members, and providers seeking information or assistance related to long-term care, assisted living, and nursing facilities in the state.

The primary authority is the **Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC)**, which consolidates many of the state's health and human services functions, including Medicaid administration and facility regulation [1].

## VI. The Importance of Using Verified Contacts

In the complex world of long-term care, using verified, official contacts is paramount for several reasons:

1.  **Accuracy of Information:** Official agencies provide the most current and accurate information regarding eligibility, regulations, and program changes.
2.  **Confidentiality and Security:** Government agencies are bound by strict privacy laws (such as HIPAA) to protect personal and medical information.
3.  **Legal Authority:** Only the regulatory agencies (like HHSC) have the legal authority to investigate complaints, issue licenses, and enforce compliance.
4.  **Avoiding Scams:** The long-term care and veterans benefits fields are unfortunately targets for scams. Using the official contacts listed here ensures that consumers are dealing with legitimate, accredited sources [16].

For any Texas resident seeking to understand or access long-term care services, this directory provides the essential, verified contacts to begin their journey. The coordinated efforts of these state and federal authorities are designed to ensure that all Texans, especially the elderly and those with disabilities, receive the quality of care and support they deserve.
