# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Florida

Navigating the long-term care system in **Florida** requires interaction with multiple state and federal agencies. This document provides a comprehensive, verified list of the key governmental and official advocacy contacts responsible for Medicaid, licensing, consumer protection, and veterans' benefits within the state.

All contact information has been sourced from the respective official state and federal websites to ensure accuracy and reliability.

## I. Medicaid and Long-Term Care Services

The **Florida Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA)** is the primary agency responsible for administering the Statewide Medicaid Managed Care (SMMC) program, including the Long-Term Care (SMMC-LTC) component.

| Agency/Program | Function | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Florida Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA)** | State Medicaid Agency, overall SMMC administration | (877) 254-1055 | [https://ahca.myflorida.com/medicaid](https://ahca.myflorida.com/medicaid) |
| **SMMC Enrollment Helpline** | Assistance with Medicaid enrollment and plan selection | (877) 711-3662 | [https://www.flmedicaidmanagedcare.com/](https://www.flmedicaidmanagedcare.com/) |
| **Department of Children and Families (DCF)** | Determines financial eligibility for Medicaid | Varies by county | [https://myaccess.myflfamilies.com/](https://myaccess.myflfamilies.com/) |
| **Department of Elder Affairs (DOEA) - CARES** | Determines medical eligibility (Level of Care) for SMMC-LTC | (888) 831-0404 | [https://elderaffairs.org/](https://elderaffairs.org/) |

## II. Licensing, Regulation, and Complaint Administration

The **Florida Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA)** is also the sole licensing and regulatory authority for nursing homes and assisted living facilities (ALFs).

| Agency/Unit | Function | Phone Number | Official Website/Portal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AHCA Complaint Hotline** | To file a complaint against a licensed facility (Nursing Home, ALF, etc.) | **(888) 419-3456** | [https://ahca.myflorida.com/contact-ahca/file-a-complaint](https://ahca.myflorida.com/contact-ahca/file-a-complaint) |
| **AHCA Long Term Care Services Unit** | Licensing and regulation of Nursing Homes and ALFs | (850) 412-4303 | [https://ahca.myflorida.com/health-quality-assurance/bureau-of-health-facility-regulation/long-term-care-services-unit](https://ahca.myflorida.com/health-quality-assurance/bureau-of-health-facility-regulation/long-term-care-services-unit) |
| **Florida Health Finder** | Public access to facility inspection reports and ratings | N/A | [https://quality.healthfinder.fl.gov/](https://quality.healthfinder.fl.gov/) |

## III. Ombudsman and Advocacy Services

The **Florida Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** is the official, state-sanctioned advocate for residents' rights and quality of life in long-term care facilities.

| Agency/Program | Function | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Florida Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** | Resident advocacy, complaint investigation, and mediation | **(888) 831-0404** | [https://ombudsman.elderaffairs.org/](https://ombudsman.elderaffairs.org/) |
| **DCF Abuse Hotline** | To report suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult | **1-800-96-ABUSE (1-800-962-2873)** | [https://reportabuse.dcf.state.fl.us/](https://reportabuse.dcf.state.fl.us/) |

## IV. Veterans' Benefits and Long-Term Care

The **Florida Department of Veterans' Affairs (FDVA)** is the state agency dedicated to helping veterans access their earned benefits, including long-term care options.

| Agency/Program | Function | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Florida Department of Veterans' Affairs (FDVA)** | State veterans' benefits and services, VSO assistance | **(844) 693-5838** | [https://www.floridavets.org/](https://www.floridavets.org/) |
| **FDVA State Veterans' Homes** | Administration of the nine State Veterans' Homes | (727) 518-3202 (Main Office) | [https://floridavets.org/locations/state-veterans-nursing-homes/](https://floridavets.org/locations/state-veterans-nursing-homes/) |
| **U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)** | Federal benefits, including Aid & Attendance Pension | 1-800-827-1000 | [https://www.va.gov/](https://www.va.gov/) |
