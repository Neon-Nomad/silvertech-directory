# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Iowa: State Agencies and Key Resources

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, Medicaid, licensing, and veterans' benefits in Iowa requires direct access to the correct state authorities. This guide provides a comprehensive, verified directory of the essential government agencies and programs responsible for oversight, financial assistance, advocacy, and support for Iowans in long-term care settings.

The information below is organized by agency, providing headquarters addresses, key phone numbers, and relevant website links for the **Iowa Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)**, the **Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing (DIAL)**, the **Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman (LTCO)**, and the **Iowa Department of Veterans Affairs (IDVA)**.

The **Iowa Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)** is the umbrella agency responsible for the state's Medicaid program, Adult Protective Services, and aging services, including the Long-Term Care Ombudsman.

This verified contact directory serves as an indispensable resource for Iowa residents, family members, and caregivers seeking information, assistance, or resolution regarding long-term care services.
