# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Alabama

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, Medicaid, licensing, and advocacy in Alabama requires direct access to the correct state agencies and official programs. This guide provides a comprehensive, verified list of essential contact information for the key governmental and state-sponsored authorities that oversee long-term care services, resident rights, and veterans' benefits in Alabama.

All contact details have been sourced directly from the respective state agency websites to ensure accuracy and reliability.

## I. Medicaid and Long-Term Care Programs

The **Alabama Medicaid Agency** is the primary authority for administering the state's Medicaid program, including the various Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers that provide alternatives to institutional care.
