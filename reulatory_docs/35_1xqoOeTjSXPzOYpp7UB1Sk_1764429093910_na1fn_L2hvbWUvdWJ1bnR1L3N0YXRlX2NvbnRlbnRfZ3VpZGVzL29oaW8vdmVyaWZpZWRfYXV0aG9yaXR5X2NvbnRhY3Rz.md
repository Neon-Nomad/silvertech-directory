# Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Long-Term Care in Ohio

Navigating the long-term care system in Ohio requires connecting with a variety of state agencies, advocacy groups, and local resources. This document provides a comprehensive, verified directory of the most essential authority contacts for Medicaid, licensing, consumer advocacy, and veterans' benefits. These contacts are the primary gateways to information, assistance, and formal complaint processes within the state of Ohio.

## I. Medicaid and Long-Term Care Program Administration

The **Ohio Department of Medicaid (ODM)** and the **Ohio Department of Aging (ODA)** are the two primary state agencies responsible for administering long-term care programs, including the state's Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers.

### A. Ohio Department of Medicaid (ODM)

The ODM manages the state's Medicaid program, including financial eligibility and the overall structure of long-term care coverage.

### C. Federal Resource Deep Dive: Medicare and VA

#### Medicare Nursing Home Compare

The **Medicare Nursing Home Compare** website is an indispensable tool for consumers. It is the official source for the federal **Five-Star Quality Rating System** for nursing homes.

*   **Data Points:** The site provides data on health inspections, staffing levels (Registered Nurse hours per resident day), and quality measures (e.g., rates of pressure ulcers, use of antipsychotic medications).
*   **Utility:** Consumers should use this site to compare facilities side-by-side and review the full text of the most recent inspection reports before making a placement decision.

#### U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA)

For all federal benefits, the VA's main website is the authoritative source.

*   **VA Health Care:** Veterans can find information on enrollment, eligibility, and the location of VA Community Living Centers (nursing homes) and other long-term care services.
*   **Financial Benefits:** Detailed information and forms for the **Aid and Attendance (A&A)** benefit, which is crucial for offsetting the cost of private long-term care.

By providing this multi-layered directory—from state agencies to local offices and federal comparison tools—this guide ensures that Ohio residents have a complete and verified set of contacts to navigate the complexities of long-term care.
