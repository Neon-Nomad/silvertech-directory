# Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Long-Term Care in the District of Columbia

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, Medicaid, licensing, and advocacy in the District of Columbia requires direct access to the correct governmental and non-profit authorities. This comprehensive guide compiles the verified contact information for the most critical agencies and programs, ensuring residents, family members, and caregivers can quickly connect with the resources they need.

This document serves as a single, reliable reference point for all official contacts related to long-term care services, regulation, and support in the District of Columbia.

## I. Medicaid and Long-Term Care Services

The **Department of Health Care Finance (DHCF)** is the agency responsible for administering the DC Medicaid program, including the **Elderly and Persons with Physical Disabilities (EPD) Waiver** for home and community-based services.

| Medicaid Website | **https://dhcf.dc.gov/service/medicaid** | Official website for all DC Medicaid programs. | [1] |
| **Long-Term Care Administration (LTCA)** | | | [2] |
| LTCA Hotline | Phone: **(202) 442-9533** | For questions regarding Medicaid Long-Term Care services, including the EPD Waiver. | [2] |
| LTCA Complaints | Email: **DHCFLTCAComplaints@dc.gov** | For written complaints and inquiries to the Long-Term Care Administration. | [2] |

## II. Licensing, Regulation, and Facility Complaints

The **Health Regulation and Licensing Administration (HRLA)**, under **DC Health**, is the regulatory body responsible for licensing and inspecting long-term care facilities, as well as investigating complaints of regulatory violations.

| Facility Licensing Website | **https://dchealth.dc.gov/service/health-care-facilities** | Official resource for licensing boards and health care facility information. | [5] |

## III. Ombudsman and Advocacy Services

The **Office of the D.C. Long-Term Care Ombudsman** provides independent advocacy for residents of nursing homes and assisted living facilities. The **Legal Counsel for the Elderly (LCE)** provides free legal services to seniors.

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose | Verified Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Office of the D.C. Long-Term Care Ombudsman** | | | [7] |
| Ombudsman Phone | Phone: **(202) 434-2190** | To file a confidential complaint regarding resident rights, quality of life, or discharge issues. | [7] |
| Ombudsman Email | Email: **DCOmbuds@aarp.org** | For non-urgent inquiries and general information. | [7] |
| Administering Agency | **Legal Counsel for the Elderly (LCE) / AARP Foundation** | The organization that houses and supports the Ombudsman program. | [7] |
| **LCE Legal Hotline** | Phone: **(202) 434-2120** | For free legal advice and representation for DC residents aged 60 and older. | [8] |
| **Disability Rights DC (DRDC)** | Phone: **(202) 408-3100** | Protection and advocacy for the rights of people with disabilities. | [9] |
