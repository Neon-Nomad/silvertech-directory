# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Utah

Navigating the long-term care system in Utah requires interacting with several key state agencies, each responsible for a distinct aspect of regulation, funding, and advocacy. This comprehensive guide compiles the verified contact information for the most critical state authorities, ensuring that residents, family members, and caregivers can quickly and accurately reach the correct department for their specific needs, whether it involves Medicaid eligibility, facility licensing, or resident advocacy.

## II. Licensing, Regulation, and Complaint Investigation

The **Division of Licensing and Background Checks (DLBC)**, under the DHHS, is the regulatory body responsible for licensing and inspecting all long-term care facilities, including nursing homes and assisted living facilities. This is the primary contact for regulatory complaints.

| Agency | Utah Division of Licensing and Background Checks (DLBC) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Licensing, inspection, and enforcement of state and federal regulations for health facilities. Investigation of regulatory complaints. |
| **Website** | [https://dlbc.utah.gov/](https://dlbc.utah.gov/) [7] |
| **General Phone** | **(801) 538-4242** [8] |
| **Complaint Submission** | **Online Form** via the "Submit a Concern" page [9] |
| **Mailing Address** | Multi-Agency State Office Building, 195 North 1950 West, Salt Lake City, Utah 84116 [8] |

### Key DLBC Contact Points and Functions

The DLBC is structured to handle different aspects of facility oversight. When contacting the DLBC, it is helpful to know the specific function you are seeking:

*   **Licensing Inquiries:** For questions about a facility's current license status, ownership, or history of compliance.
*   **Complaint Intake:** For reporting a violation of state or federal regulations (e.g., staffing issues, poor sanitation, medication errors). The DLBC maintains a dedicated complaint intake process to ensure all allegations are properly documented and investigated.
*   **Background Checks:** The DLBC also manages the background check process for all employees of licensed healthcare facilities, a critical function for ensuring resident safety.

### The Role of the Disability Law Center (DLC)

The DLC is a non-profit organization designated by the Governor to protect the rights of Utahns with disabilities. In the context of long-term care, the DLC is a crucial resource for residents who face systemic discrimination, rights violations, or complex legal challenges that are beyond the scope of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman.

**Key Services Provided by the DLC:**

*   **Legal Representation:** Providing direct legal services and representation in cases involving abuse, neglect, or rights violations in facilities.
*   **Systemic Advocacy:** Working to change laws, policies, and practices that negatively affect people with disabilities.
*   **Information and Referral:** Offering advice and connecting individuals with other resources.
*   **Involuntary Discharge Appeals:** The DLC often partners with the Ombudsman to represent residents in administrative hearings to appeal involuntary transfers or discharges from nursing homes.

## VI. Medicaid Fraud and Abuse Reporting

While the DLBC handles regulatory complaints and APS handles vulnerable adult abuse, allegations of fraud or misuse of public funds must be directed to specialized units.

### Medicaid Fraud Control Unit (MFCU)

The **Medicaid Fraud Control Unit (MFCU)**, housed within the Utah Attorney General's Office, is responsible for investigating and prosecuting fraud committed by Medicaid providers (e.g., facilities, doctors, home health agencies) and for investigating and prosecuting instances of abuse or neglect of Medicaid patients in healthcare facilities.

| Agency | Medicaid Fraud Control Unit (MFCU) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Investigates and prosecutes provider fraud and patient abuse/neglect in facilities. |
| **Hotline (Provider Fraud)** | **(855) 403-7283** (Utah Office of Inspector General) [17] |
| **Hotline (Patient Abuse/Neglect)** | **(801) 538-4242** (DHHS/DLBC main line, ask for MFCU) [8] |

### Member Fraud Reporting

Allegations of fraud committed by Medicaid members (e.g., misrepresenting income or assets to gain eligibility) are handled by the Department of Workforce Services (DWS).

| Agency | Department of Workforce Services (DWS) Fraud Hotline |
| :--- | :--- |

### AAA Services

The AAAs provide a range of services that are invaluable for long-term care planning:

*   **Information and Referral (I&R):** Providing a single point of entry for all aging and disability services in their region.
*   **Caregiver Support:** Offering training, support groups, and respite care for family caregivers.
*   **Nutrition Services:** Administering the Meals on Wheels program and congregate meal sites.
*   **Case Management:** Providing personalized assistance to help seniors access and coordinate services.
*   **Legal Assistance:** Connecting seniors with legal aid services for issues like wills, powers of attorney, and public benefits.
