# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Mississippi

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care in Mississippi requires access to accurate and reliable information from the state's official governing and advocacy bodies. This document provides a consolidated and verified list of essential contact information for the key state agencies responsible for Medicaid, facility licensing, resident advocacy, and complaint resolution.

This resource is designed to serve as a quick reference for residents, family members, and caregivers seeking to connect with the appropriate Mississippi state authority.

## III. Detailed Agency Roles

### Mississippi Division of Medicaid (DOM)

The DOM is the single state agency responsible for the administration of the Medicaid program. For long-term care, the DOM manages the financial eligibility process and oversees the various Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers, such as the Assisted Living (AL) Waiver, the Elderly and Disabled (E&D) Waiver, and the Traumatic Brain/Spinal Cord Injury (TBI/SCI) Waiver. Contacting the DOM is essential for questions regarding Medicaid eligibility, covered services, and enrollment in waiver programs.

### Mississippi State Department of Health (MSDH)

The MSDH, through its licensure division, is the regulatory watchdog for all health facilities. Its primary role is to conduct unannounced inspections (surveys) and investigate complaints to ensure facilities comply with state licensing laws and federal certification requirements for Medicare and Medicaid. The MSDH is the authority that can issue citations, fines, and other enforcement actions against non-compliant facilities.

### State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

The Ombudsman program is an independent advocacy service. It is a confidential resource for residents who have concerns about their rights, quality of life, or care. The Ombudsman works to resolve issues through mediation and negotiation, and also educates residents and staff on resident rights. The Ombudsman does not have regulatory power but is a powerful voice for residents.

### Mississippi Veterans Affairs Board (MSVA)

The MSVA is the state-level resource for veterans' benefits. In the context of long-term care, the MSVA manages the four State Veterans Homes, which provide high-quality, subsidized skilled nursing care for eligible veterans and their spouses. The MSVA also assists veterans in accessing a wide range of state and federal benefits, including property tax exemptions and educational assistance.
