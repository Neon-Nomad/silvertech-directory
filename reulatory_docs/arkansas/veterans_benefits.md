# Arkansas Veterans Benefits: A Comprehensive Guide to State Programs and Services

The State of Arkansas, often referred to as the "Natural State," is committed to honoring the service and sacrifice of its military veterans and their families. The **Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs (ADVA)** serves as the central agency, providing advocacy and education to connect veterans and their eligible dependents with the state and federal benefits they have earned [1].

Arkansas offers a robust package of state-specific benefits, including significant tax exemptions, educational assistance, employment preferences, and high-quality long-term care through its State Veterans Homes. This comprehensive guide details the key benefits available to Arkansas veterans, their eligibility requirements, and how to access them.

| Benefit Category | Key Arkansas Benefit | Administering Agency |

## Tax Benefits for Arkansas Veterans

Arkansas provides some of the most significant tax advantages for veterans in the nation, offering relief on both income and property taxes.

### 1. Military Retirement Pay Exemption

Arkansas fully exempts **military retirement pay** from state income tax [2]. This benefit applies to all retired military personnel, regardless of age or disability status, and is a major financial advantage for veterans choosing to reside in the state.

### 2. Disabled Veteran Property Tax Exemption

One of the most valuable benefits is the **property tax exemption** for disabled veterans.

*   **Eligibility:** A veteran must be a resident of Arkansas and have been rated by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) as having a **100% service-connected disability** [3].
*   **Benefit:** The veteran is exempt from all state, county, and local property taxes on their **homestead** (primary residence) and **personal property**.
*   **Surviving Spouse Benefit:** The exemption is also extended to the surviving spouse and minor dependent children of a 100% disabled veteran, provided the spouse does not remarry. The exemption also applies to the surviving spouse of a service member who was killed in action.

### 3. Income Tax Exemption for Active Duty Pay

Active duty military pay and allowances are also exempt from Arkansas state income tax, effective January 1, 2014 [4]. This exemption applies to all active duty service members, including those serving in the National Guard or Reserves on active duty orders.

## Healthcare and Long-Term Care

The ADVA is responsible for overseeing the state's long-term care facilities dedicated to veterans, ensuring they receive high-quality skilled nursing care.

### Arkansas State Veterans Homes

Arkansas operates two State Veterans Homes, providing a comfortable, dignified, and supportive environment for veterans who require long-term skilled nursing care.

*   **Locations:**
    1.  **Arkansas State Veterans Home at Fayetteville**
    2.  **Arkansas State Veterans Home at North Little Rock**
*   **Services:** These homes provide 24-hour skilled nursing care, physician services, physical and occupational therapy, dietary services, social services, and a variety of recreational activities.
*   **Eligibility for Admission:**
    *   The applicant must be an honorably discharged veteran.
    *   The applicant must be an Arkansas resident (residents have first priority).
    *   The applicant must have a medical need for skilled nursing home care, as determined by a physician.
    *   **Priority Admission:** Veterans with a 70% or greater service-connected disability rating are often given priority in admissions [5].
    *   **Spouses and Gold Star Parents:** Spouses of eligible veterans and Gold Star Parents (parents of a service member who died while serving) may also be eligible for admission on a space-available basis.

The cost of care is typically covered by a combination of the VA, Medicaid, Medicare, and the resident's personal funds, with the VA covering a significant portion for service-connected disabilities.

## Education Benefits

Arkansas provides substantial educational assistance to veterans and, notably, to the dependents of veterans who have made the ultimate sacrifice or suffered significant service-connected disabilities.

### Military Dependents Scholarship (MDS) Program

The MDS Program is the flagship state education benefit, administered by the Arkansas Department of Higher Education (ADHE).

*   **Eligibility:** The program provides financial aid for the children and spouses of Arkansas veterans who meet specific criteria:
    *   The veteran must have been an Arkansas resident at the time of entry into service or have been a resident for at least one year immediately prior to the time of application.
    *   The veteran must be classified as **100% permanently and totally disabled** by the VA, **Missing in Action (MIA)**, **Prisoner of War (POW)**, or **killed in action** [6].
*   **Benefit:** The scholarship covers **free tuition and mandatory fees** at any state-supported college, university, technical school, or vocational school in Arkansas.
*   **Duration:** The benefit is available for up to 8 semesters (4 years) of undergraduate study.

### Other Educational Support

*   **In-State Tuition:** Arkansas grants in-state tuition rates to all honorably discharged veterans, their spouses, and their dependents who reside in the state, regardless of the length of time they have lived there.
*   **High School Diploma:** Veterans who left high school to serve in World War II, the Korean War, or the Vietnam War may be eligible to receive a high school diploma from their local school district.

## Employment and Professional Benefits

Arkansas recognizes the value of military service by offering preference in public employment and support for professional licensing.

### Veterans Preference in Public Employment

The **Arkansas Veterans Preference Law** mandates that preference be given to qualified veterans, their spouses, or surviving spouses in hiring and promotion for state and local government jobs [7].

*   **Preference Points:** Veterans who meet the minimum qualifications for a position are typically awarded preference points added to their examination score or are given priority consideration in the hiring process.
*   **Eligibility:** Preference is generally granted to honorably discharged veterans who served during a period of conflict or who have a service-connected disability. Spouses and unremarried surviving spouses of disabled or deceased veterans are also eligible.
*   **Reemployment Rights:** State employees who leave their positions to serve in the military are guaranteed reemployment rights upon their honorable discharge, in accordance with state and federal law.

### Professional Licensing and Certification

Arkansas works to ease the transition of military personnel and their spouses into the civilian workforce by recognizing military training and experience for professional licensing.

*   **Expedited Licensing:** State licensing boards are often required to expedite the issuance of professional licenses or certifications for active duty service members, veterans, and their spouses who hold a current license in another state.
*   **Credit for Military Training:** Military education, training, and experience may be accepted in lieu of some civilian requirements for professional licenses, such as those for emergency medical technicians, nurses, or commercial drivers.

## Other Key State Benefits

Beyond the major categories, Arkansas offers several other benefits designed to improve the quality of life for its veteran community.

### Hunting and Fishing Licenses

*   **Disabled Veterans:** Veterans with a service-connected disability rating of 100% are eligible for a **free lifetime hunting and fishing license** [8].
*   **Other Veterans:** Reduced-fee licenses are available for other disabled veterans and senior veterans.

### Special License Plates

Arkansas offers a wide variety of **specialty license plates** to honor veterans, including plates for Purple Heart recipients, former Prisoners of War (POW), disabled veterans, and various branches of the military. Many of these plates are available at no additional cost or a reduced fee.

### Veterans Cemeteries

The ADVA oversees the operation of the **Arkansas State Veterans Cemetery** in North Little Rock, providing a dignified final resting place for eligible veterans, their spouses, and dependent children.

*   **Eligibility:** Eligibility is generally the same as for burial in a national cemetery, requiring an honorable discharge.
*   **Cost:** There is typically no cost for the veteran's interment, though a fee may apply for the burial of a spouse or dependent.

## Accessing Your Benefits: The Veteran Service Officer (VSO) Network

The most effective way for an Arkansas veteran to access their earned benefits is through the **Veteran Service Officer (VSO) Network** managed by the ADVA.

*   **Role of the VSO:** VSOs are trained and accredited professionals who provide free assistance to veterans and their families. They can:
    *   Explain the full range of state and federal benefits.
    *   Assist with filing claims for VA disability compensation, pension, and healthcare.
    *   Represent the veteran in appeals before the VA.
    *   Connect veterans with local resources and support services.
*   **District Offices:** The ADVA has implemented a network of District Veteran Service Officers across the state to provide direct support to County Veteran Service Officers, ensuring local access to expert assistance.

Veterans are strongly encouraged to contact their local County VSO to begin the process of claiming their benefits.

## Conclusion

The State of Arkansas demonstrates its commitment to its military community through a comprehensive suite of benefits that address financial, healthcare, educational, and employment needs. From the **full exemption of military retirement pay** from state income tax to the provision of **two State Veterans Homes** and **free tuition for dependents** through the MDS program, Arkansas provides a supportive environment for those who have served. The **Arkansas Department of Veterans Affairs** and its **Veteran Service Officer Network** stand ready to ensure that every eligible veteran and their family can access the benefits they have rightfully earned.

## In-Depth: Detailed Eligibility and Application Processes

To provide a truly comprehensive guide, a closer look at the specific eligibility criteria and application procedures for the most sought-after benefits is necessary.

### Property Tax Exemption: Detailed Eligibility

The 100% disabled veteran property tax exemption is a cornerstone benefit. The key to eligibility is the VA's determination of **100% service-connected disability**.

*   **VA Rating:** The veteran must possess a letter from the VA confirming a 100% service-connected disability rating. This includes veterans who are rated less than 100% but are deemed **"unemployable"** by the VA, which is considered equivalent to 100% for this benefit.
*   **Homestead Definition:** The exemption applies to the veteran's primary residence and up to one acre of land. If the property is larger than one acre, the veteran is still exempt from the tax on the first acre.
*   **Application Process:** The veteran must apply for the exemption at their local **County Assessor's Office**. They must present their VA award letter and a copy of their discharge papers (DD-214). The exemption must be applied for annually, though the VA letter only needs to be submitted once unless the rating changes.

### Military Dependents Scholarship (MDS) Application

The MDS program is a life-changing educational benefit for dependents. The application process is managed by the Arkansas Department of Higher Education (ADHE).

1.  **Veteran Status Verification:** The veteran's status (100% disabled, MIA, POW, or Killed in Action) must be verified by the ADVA. The dependent must obtain a letter of eligibility from the ADVA.
2.  **ADHE Application:** The dependent must complete the MDS application through the ADHE website. The application typically opens in the spring for the following academic year.
3.  **FAFSA Requirement:** Applicants are generally required to complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), as the MDS is often a "last-dollar" scholarship, covering the remaining tuition and fees after other federal and state aid has been applied.
4.  **Enrollment:** The dependent must be accepted and enrolled in an approved state-supported institution in an undergraduate program.

The benefit is a powerful tool for ensuring the children and spouses of Arkansas's most severely impacted veterans can achieve their educational goals without the burden of tuition costs.

### Veterans Preference in State Employment: The Law

The Arkansas Veterans Preference Law (Arkansas Code § 21-3-302) is not merely a suggestion; it is a legally mandated preference.

*   **Types of Preference:**
    *   **5-Point Preference:** Awarded to any honorably discharged veteran who served during a period of conflict.
    *   **10-Point Preference:** Awarded to veterans with a service-connected disability, or the unremarried spouse of a deceased veteran who died while on active duty or as a result of a service-connected disability.
*   **Application:** To claim preference, the veteran must submit a copy of their DD-214 and, if applicable, their VA disability rating letter with their job application. The preference is applied only when the veteran meets the minimum qualifications for the position.
