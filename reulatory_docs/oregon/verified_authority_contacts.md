## VI. Key Findings and Summary of Authority

Oregon's long-term care infrastructure is characterized by a strong emphasis on community-based care, coordinated by the ODHS and OHA. The state's commitment to veterans is highlighted by the operation of two state-run Veterans' Homes.

> **Key Findings Summary:**
> *   **Medicaid Program:** The **Oregon Health Plan (OHP)** is administered by the **Oregon Health Authority (OHA)**.
> *   **Licensing Authority:** The **Oregon Department of Human Services (ODHS)**, specifically the Aging and People with Disabilities (APD) division, licenses all long-term care facilities.
> *   **Advocacy:** The **Office of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman (OLTCO)** is the independent advocate for residents' rights.
> *   **Veterans' Services:** The **Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs (ODVA)** is the central agency for state veterans' benefits.
> *   **Complaint Hotline:** The state maintains a dedicated complaint hotline for residential care and assisted living facilities at **844-503-4773** and a separate line for nursing facilities at **877-280-4555**.

This comprehensive list of verified contacts serves as a crucial resource for Oregon residents, ensuring direct access to the authoritative sources for all long-term care and benefits inquiries.
