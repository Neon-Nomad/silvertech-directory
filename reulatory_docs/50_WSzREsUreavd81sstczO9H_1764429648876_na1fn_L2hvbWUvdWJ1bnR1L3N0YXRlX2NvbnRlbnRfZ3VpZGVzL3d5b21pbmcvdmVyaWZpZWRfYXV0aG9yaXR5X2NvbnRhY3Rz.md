# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Wyoming

## Introduction: Navigating Wyoming's Senior Care Resources

Securing and managing long-term care services can be a complex process. In Wyoming, several key state agencies and programs are responsible for overseeing healthcare, licensing facilities, protecting resident rights, and providing benefits to eligible citizens, particularly seniors and veterans. This guide consolidates the verified contact information for the most critical state authorities, ensuring that residents, family members, and caregivers can quickly access the right resources for assistance, advocacy, and information.

The primary authorities in Wyoming for long-term care are centralized within the **Wyoming Department of Health** and the **Wyoming Veterans Commission**. Understanding the specific role of each entity is the first step in effectively navigating the state's support system.

## Detailed Authority Profiles

### 1. Wyoming Medicaid

**Agency:** Wyoming Department of Health, Division of Healthcare Financing

**Mission and Role:**
Wyoming Medicaid is a joint federal and state program that provides healthcare coverage to eligible low-income and medically needy residents. For the senior population, its most critical function is funding long-term services and supports (LTSS), including nursing home care and home and community-based services (HCBS). The program is essential for those who require assistance with daily living activities and medical care but cannot afford the high costs of long-term care.

**Key Program: Community Choices Waiver (CCW)**
The CCW is Wyoming's HCBS waiver that allows eligible individuals to receive nursing facility level of care in a community setting, such as an assisted living facility or their own home. The Medicaid office is the primary contact for all questions regarding eligibility, application status, and benefits for the CCW and other Medicaid programs.

### 2. Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS)

**Agency:** Wyoming Department of Health, Aging Division

**Mission and Role:**
The Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS) division is the state's regulatory arm for healthcare facilities. Its mission is to assure that residents and patients receive quality care by enforcing state and federal regulations. HLS is responsible for:
*   **Licensing:** Issuing and renewing licenses for facilities such as Nursing Care Facilities, Assisted Living Facilities, and Hospitals.
*   **Inspections:** Conducting unannounced, periodic on-site surveys to monitor compliance with health, safety, and quality standards.
*   **Complaint Investigation:** Serving as the official state agency for investigating formal complaints against licensed healthcare facilities.

**Facilities Regulated:**
HLS regulates a broad spectrum of facilities, ensuring that all licensed providers meet minimum standards of care. This includes, but is not limited to, Nursing Care Facilities, Assisted Living Facilities, Adult Day Care Facilities, and Home Health Agencies.

### 3. Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

**Agency:** Wyoming Department of Health (Administratively housed)

**Mission and Role:**
The Wyoming Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP) is an independent advocacy program dedicated to protecting the rights, health, safety, and welfare of residents in long-term care facilities and recipients of community-based long-term care services. The Ombudsman acts as a neutral party, mediating disputes and advocating for the resident's wishes.

**Key Services:**
*   **Complaint Resolution:** Investigating and resolving complaints made by or on behalf of residents.
*   **Resident Rights Advocacy:** Educating residents and the public about resident rights and intervening when rights are violated.
*   **Confidentiality:** All services are confidential and provided at no cost.

**Note on Contact:** The Ombudsman's toll-free number is the most direct way for residents and family members to report concerns and seek advocacy.

### 4. HLS Complaint Process and Hotline

**Agency:** Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS)

**Mission and Role:**
While HLS has a general contact number, a dedicated toll-free hotline is provided specifically for reporting serious concerns and formal complaints against licensed healthcare facilities. This hotline ensures that allegations of abuse, neglect, or serious regulatory violations are immediately routed to the appropriate investigative unit.

**Filing a Complaint:**
Complaints can be filed anonymously, and the identity of the complainant is kept confidential. The HLS team will triage the complaint and dispatch surveyors for an unannounced on-site investigation, especially for high-priority cases involving immediate jeopardy to a resident's health or safety.

**Note on Distinction:** The HLS Complaint Hotline is for formal, regulatory complaints, while the Ombudsman's number is for advocacy and mediation. Both are valuable resources, and individuals may contact both depending on the nature of their concern.

### 5. Wyoming Veterans Commission

**Agency:** Wyoming Veterans Commission

**Mission and Role:**
The Wyoming Veterans Commission is the state's central resource for veterans' benefits and services. The Commission's primary role is to assist veterans, their families, and survivors in obtaining all federal and state benefits they are entitled to. This includes assistance with VA claims, state tax exemptions, and access to the state's veterans' home.

**Key Services:**
*   **Veterans Service Officers (VSOs):** Providing free, expert assistance with filing and managing VA claims.
*   **State Benefits:** Information and assistance with the Veterans Property Tax Exemption and other state-specific programs.
*   **Veterans' Home of Wyoming:** Oversight and information regarding the state's long-term care facility for veterans in Buffalo.

**Note on Contact:** The toll-free number is the best way to connect with a Veterans Service Officer for personalized assistance.
