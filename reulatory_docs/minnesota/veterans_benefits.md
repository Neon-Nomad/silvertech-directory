# Comprehensive Guide to Veterans Benefits and Long-Term Care in Minnesota

Minnesota is deeply committed to honoring its veterans, offering a wide array of state-specific benefits that supplement federal programs. The **Minnesota Department of Veterans Affairs (MDVA)** is the principal agency responsible for connecting veterans and their families with these crucial resources, which span financial assistance, education, employment, and, most notably, long-term care [1].

A key highlight of Minnesota’s commitment is its extensive network of **State Veterans Homes**, which provide high-quality, long-term skilled nursing care and assisted living services. With eight such facilities across the state, Minnesota offers one of the most robust state-run long-term care systems for veterans in the nation [2]. This guide provides a detailed overview of the long-term care and financial benefits available to Minnesota veterans, their spouses, and dependents.

## I. The Minnesota Department of Veterans Affairs (MDVA)

The MDVA serves as the central hub for all state veterans' services. Its mission is to serve Minnesota veterans and their families with dignity and respect by providing a wide range of benefits and services.

### A. Key Services Provided by MDVA

The MDVA provides direct services and oversight in several critical areas:

*   **Veterans Service Officers (VSOs):** The MDVA works closely with County Veterans Service Officers (CVSOs) in every county to provide personalized assistance with filing claims for federal VA benefits, including disability compensation, pension, and healthcare.
*   **Financial Assistance:** Administering state programs that provide direct financial aid to veterans and their families facing temporary financial hardship.
*   **Outreach and Advocacy:** Conducting outreach to connect with underserved veterans and advocating for state and federal legislative changes to improve veterans' lives.
*   **State Veterans Homes:** Operating and managing the state’s network of long-term care facilities.

## II. Minnesota State Veterans Homes: A Commitment to Long-Term Care

Minnesota operates eight State Veterans Homes, offering a continuum of care that includes skilled nursing, memory care, and domiciliary (assisted living) services. This extensive network is a cornerstone of the state's veterans' benefits package.

### A. Locations of Minnesota Veterans Homes

Minnesota’s eight State Veterans Homes are strategically located across the state to ensure accessibility for veterans and their families [2]:

2.  **Hastings**

5.  **Preston**
6.  **Silver Bay**
7.  **St. Cloud**

### B. Eligibility and Admission Criteria

Admission to a Minnesota Veterans Home is governed by state law and administrative rules. The eligibility criteria are specific and must be met by all applicants [3]:

*   **Veteran Status:** The applicant must be a veteran who was discharged under honorable conditions.
*   **Service Requirement:** The veteran must have served for at least 181 consecutive days on active duty, or have been discharged sooner due to a service-connected disability.
*   **Residency:** The veteran must be a resident of Minnesota at the time of application, or have had their service credited to Minnesota.
*   **Medical Need:** All applicants must demonstrate a medical need for the level of care provided (skilled nursing, memory care, or assisted living).

### C. Spouses and Gold Star Families

In a significant state benefit, the spouses of eligible veterans may also be admitted to a State Veterans Home, provided they meet certain criteria, typically including being 55 years of age or older and meeting the medical need requirement. The MDVA also extends services to Gold Star families, recognizing the sacrifices of those who lost a loved one in service [3].

### D. Cost of Care and Financial Assistance

The cost of care in a State Veterans Home is based on the resident’s ability to pay, with federal and state benefits often covering a significant portion of the expense.

*   **Federal VA Per Diem:** The federal Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) provides a per diem payment to the state for each eligible veteran resident, which substantially reduces the out-of-pocket cost.
*   **Medicaid (Medical Assistance):** For veterans who meet the financial eligibility requirements, Minnesota’s Medical Assistance (MA) program can cover the remaining cost of skilled nursing care.
*   **Private Pay:** Residents who do not qualify for federal or state assistance are responsible for the full cost of care, though the MDVA works to ensure no veteran is denied care solely due to an inability to pay.

## III. State Financial Assistance Programs

Beyond long-term care, the MDVA administers several state-funded programs designed to provide direct financial relief and support to veterans and their families.

### A. Subsistence Assistance Program

The **Subsistence Assistance Program** is designed to provide temporary financial assistance to veterans or their surviving spouses who are disabled and facing a short-term financial crisis. The purpose is to help maintain the basic needs of the veteran and their family, such as rent, utilities, and food [4].

### B. Special Needs Grant

The **Special Needs Grant** is a one-time grant available to veterans and their dependents who have an emergency need that cannot be met through other resources. This grant can cover a variety of unexpected expenses, including:

*   **Medical and Dental Expenses:** Costs not covered by insurance or other benefits.
*   **Home Repair:** Emergency repairs to maintain a safe living environment.
*   **Transportation:** Costs associated with necessary medical appointments or employment.

### C. Veterans Assistance Fund (VAF)

The MDVA also oversees the **Veterans Assistance Fund (VAF)**, which provides financial aid for a variety of needs, often in partnership with local Veterans Service Organizations (VSOs) like the American Legion and VFW. This fund is crucial for bridging gaps in federal and other state assistance programs [5].

## IV. Other Key State Benefits for Veterans

Minnesota offers a range of other benefits that enhance the lives of veterans and their families.

### A. Property Tax Exclusion

Minnesota provides a significant property tax exclusion for the homesteads of disabled veterans. The amount of the exclusion varies based on the veteran’s level of service-connected disability, with a 100% exclusion for veterans with a 100% service-connected disability [6].

### B. Education Benefits

The **Minnesota GI Bill** is a state-funded program that provides financial assistance for education, on-the-job training, and apprenticeships to eligible veterans, service members, and their dependents. This benefit is designed to supplement the federal Post-9/11 GI Bill and can be used for a variety of educational pursuits [7].

### C. Employment Preference

Veterans receive preference in hiring and promotion for state and local government jobs in Minnesota. This preference is a significant advantage in the competitive public sector job market.

### D. Hunting and Fishing Licenses

Minnesota offers reduced-fee or free hunting and fishing licenses to disabled veterans and those who are currently serving in the military.

## V. Connecting with a County Veterans Service Officer (CVSO)

The most effective way for a veteran to access their benefits is by connecting with their local **County Veterans Service Officer (CVSO)**. The CVSO is a trained professional employed by the county who is accredited by the VA to assist veterans with filing claims and navigating the complex benefits system.

*   **CVSO Role:**
    *   Assisting with applications for federal VA disability compensation, pension, and healthcare.
    *   Providing information and application assistance for all state-level MDVA benefits.
    *   Connecting veterans with local resources, including food shelves, housing assistance, and mental health services.

The MDVA strongly encourages all veterans to establish a relationship with their CVSO as the first step in securing their entitled benefits.

## Conclusion

Minnesota stands out for its deep commitment to its veteran population, offering a comprehensive suite of benefits that address the critical needs of long-term care and financial stability.
