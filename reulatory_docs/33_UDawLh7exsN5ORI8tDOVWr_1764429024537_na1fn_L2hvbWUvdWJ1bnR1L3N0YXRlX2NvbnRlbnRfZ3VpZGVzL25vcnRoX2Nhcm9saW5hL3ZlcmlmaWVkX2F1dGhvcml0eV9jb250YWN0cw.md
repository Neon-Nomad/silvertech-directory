# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in North Carolina: A Comprehensive Directory

Accessing information and assistance within North Carolina's long-term care system requires connecting with the correct state and federal agencies. This comprehensive directory provides a verified, quick-reference list of the essential authority contacts for all major long-term care categories, including Medicaid, licensing, advocacy, and veterans' benefits.

## I. North Carolina Department of Health and Human Services (NCDHHS)

The NCDHHS is the umbrella agency overseeing health and human services in the state, including Medicaid, aging services, and regulatory oversight.

| Agency/Program | Contact Name/Section | Phone Number | Website | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NC Medicaid** | Division of Health Benefits (DHB) | (888) 245-0179 | https://medicaid.ncdhhs.gov/ | General Medicaid inquiries, eligibility, and benefits. |
| **State/County Special Assistance (SA)** | Local County Department of Social Services (DSS) | Contact Local DSS Office | https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/social-services/special-assistance | Cash supplement for room and board in Adult Care Homes. |
| **Adult Protective Services (APS)** | Local County Department of Social Services (DSS) | Contact Local DSS Office | https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/social-services/adult-services/adult-protective-services | Reporting abuse, neglect, or exploitation of disabled adults. |
| **General Information** | CARE-LINE | (800) 662-7030 | https://www.ncdhhs.gov/ | General inquiries and referral to appropriate services. |

## II. Licensing and Regulatory Oversight (DHSR)

The **Division of Health Service Regulation (DHSR)** is the state's regulatory body, responsible for licensing and inspecting all health care facilities.

| Agency/Program | Contact Name/Section | Phone Number | Website | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nursing Home Licensure** | DHSR, Nursing Home Licensure and Certification Section (NHLCS) | (919) 855-4520 | https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/nhlcs/index.html | Licensing, certification, and inspection of nursing homes. |
| **Assisted Living Licensure** | DHSR, Adult Care Licensure Section (ACLS) | (919) 855-3765 | https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/acls/index.html | Licensing and certification of Adult Care Homes and Family Care Homes. |
| **Complaint Intake Unit** | DHSR, Complaint Intake Unit (CIU) | **(800) 624-3004** (In-State Hotline) | https://info.ncdhhs.gov/dhsr/ciu/filecomplaint.html | Reporting regulatory violations, health, and safety concerns in licensed facilities. |
| **CIU Local Phone** | DHSR, Complaint Intake Unit (CIU) | (919) 855-4500 | N/A | Local contact number for the Complaint Intake Unit. |

## III. Ombudsman and Advocacy Services

The Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program provides confidential advocacy, while Disability Rights North Carolina offers legal support for individuals with disabilities.

| Agency/Program | Contact Name/Section | Phone Number | Website | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long-Term Care Ombudsman** | NC DHHS, Division of Aging and Adult Services | **(800) 662-7030** (Toll-Free) | https://www.ncdhhs.gov/divisions/aging/long-term-care-ombudsman | Confidential advocacy and grievance resolution for residents of nursing and adult care homes. |
| **Disability Rights NC** | Protection and Advocacy System | (877) 235-4210 | https://disabilityrightsnc.org/ | Legal and advocacy services for people with disabilities. |
| **Area Agencies on Aging (AAA)** | Regional Ombudsman Offices | Varies by Region | NCDHHS Ombudsman Contact List | Localized information and referral services for seniors. |

## IV. Veterans Benefits and Services

The **North Carolina Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA)** is the state agency dedicated to assisting veterans with their benefits, including access to State Veterans Homes.

| Agency/Program | Contact Name/Section | Phone Number | Website | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Veterans Affairs Agency** | NC Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) | (844) 624-8387 | https://www.milvets.nc.gov/ | General information on state and federal veterans benefits. |
| **State Veterans Homes** | DMVA, State Veterans Homes Program | Contact DMVA or Local CVSO | https://www.milvets.nc.gov/benefits-services/nc-state-veterans-homes-skilled-nursing-care | Information and application for admission to the four State Veterans Homes. |
| **County Veterans Service Officers (CVSO)** | Local County Veterans Service Office | Contact Local CVSO | https://www.milvets.nc.gov/benefits-services/benefits-claims | Free, accredited assistance with filing VA claims and accessing benefits. |

This table summarizes the key facility types, their primary regulatory oversight, and the main state-level financial assistance programs available.
