# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Connecticut

Navigating the long-term care system in Connecticut requires engaging with several key state and federal agencies. This guide provides a comprehensive, verified list of the most important governmental and advocacy contacts for Medicaid, licensing, ombudsman services, and veterans' benefits.

## I. Medicaid and Financial Assistance

The **Connecticut Department of Social Services (DSS)** is the primary agency responsible for administering the state's Medicaid program, known as HUSKY Health, and the critical Connecticut Home Care Program for Elders (CHCPE).

| Agency/Program | Purpose | Phone Number | Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Connecticut Department of Social Services (DSS)** | General administration of HUSKY Health (Medicaid) and the Connecticut Home Care Program for Elders (CHCPE). | **(800) 859-9889** (Toll-Free) | [https://portal.ct.gov/dss](https://portal.ct.gov/dss) |
| **HUSKY Health Member Services** | Information on benefits, enrollment, and finding providers within the HUSKY Health network. | **(800) 859-9889** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.huskyhealthct.org/](https://www.huskyhealthct.org/) |
| **DSS Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS) Application Centers** | For submitting the W-1LTSS application for long-term care Medicaid (nursing home or CHCPE waiver). | Varies by region; check the DSS website for the correct regional center. | [https://portal.ct.gov/dss/health-and-home-care/long-term-care/long-term-services-and-supports---ltss/apply](https://portal.ct.gov/dss/health-and-home-care/long-term-care/long-term-services-and-supports---ltss/apply) |
| **Access Health CT** | Connecticut's official health insurance marketplace, which can assist with HUSKY Health enrollment. | **(855) 805-4325** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.accesshealthct.com/](https://www.accesshealthct.com/) |

## II. Licensing, Regulation, and Complaint Investigation

The **Connecticut Department of Public Health (DPH)** is the state's regulatory authority for healthcare facilities, ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards.

| Agency/Program | Purpose | Phone Number | Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CT Department of Public Health (DPH)** | General DPH contact. | **(860) 509-7400** | [https://portal.ct.gov/dph](https://portal.ct.gov/dph) |
| **Facility Licensing and Investigations Section (FLIS)** | Licenses and regulates nursing homes, residential care homes, and Assisted Living Services Agencies (ALSAs). | **(860) 509-7400** | [https://portal.ct.gov/dph/facility-licensing--investigations/facility-licensing--investigations-section-flis/facility-licensing](https://portal.ct.gov/dph/facility-licensing--investigations/facility-licensing--investigations-section-flis/facility-licensing) |
| **DPH FLIS Complaint Hotline** | For reporting concerns, abuse, neglect, or regulatory violations in licensed healthcare facilities (e.g., nursing homes, ALSAs). | **(800) 828-9769** (Toll-Free) | [https://portal.ct.gov/dph/facility-licensing--investigations/facility-licensing--investigations-section-flis/flis-complaint-submission](https://portal.ct.gov/dph/facility-licensing--investigations/facility-licensing--investigations-section-flis/flis-complaint-submission) |
| **DPH Practitioner Licensing and Investigations Section (PLIS)** | For complaints against individual licensed health professionals (e.g., nurses, administrators). | **(860) 509-7603** | [https://portal.ct.gov/dph/practitioner-licensing--investigations/plis/practitioner-licensing--investigations-section-plis](https://portal.ct.gov/dph/practitioner-licensing--investigations/plis/practitioner-licensing--investigations-section-plis) |

## III. Ombudsman and Advocacy Services

The **Connecticut Long Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)** is an independent advocate for residents of long-term care facilities.

| Agency/Program | Purpose | Phone Number | Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CT Long Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)** | Advocates for the rights, health, and welfare of residents in nursing homes, residential care homes, and assisted living facilities. Confidential complaint resolution. | **(866) 388-1888** (Toll-Free) | [https://portal.ct.gov/ltcop](https://portal.ct.gov/ltcop) |
| **Community Ombudsman Program** | Focuses on complaints and advocacy for individuals receiving home care services, particularly through the CHCPE waiver. | **(866) 388-1888** (Toll-Free) | [https://portal.ct.gov/ltcop](https://portal.ct.gov/ltcop) |
| **2-1-1 Infoline** | Statewide information and referral service for health and human services, including elder care resources. | **2-1-1** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.211ct.org/](https://www.211ct.org/) |
