# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Massachusetts

Navigating the long-term care system in Massachusetts requires connecting with the correct state agencies and programs. This comprehensive guide provides a verified list of key authority contacts, including state agencies, regulatory bodies, and advocacy programs, ensuring that residents, families, and providers can quickly access accurate information and assistance.

All contact information is sourced directly from the official websites of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and is organized by the specific area of long-term care they oversee.

## I. Medicaid and Health Coverage (MassHealth)

**MassHealth** is the state's Medicaid program, providing health coverage and long-term care services to eligible residents. The agency is responsible for eligibility determination, benefit administration, and oversight of the Home and Community Based Services (HCBS) Waivers.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Key Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MassHealth** | **Website:** [https://www.mass.gov/orgs/masshealth](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/masshealth) | State Medicaid Program, eligibility, benefits, and HCBS Waivers. |
| **MassHealth Customer Service** | **Phone:** (800) 841-2900 (TTY: (800) 497-4648) | General inquiries, application status, and member services. |
| **MassHealth Enrollment Centers (MECs)** | **Website:** [https://www.mass.gov/masshealth-enrollment-centers](https://www.mass.gov/masshealth-enrollment-centers) | In-person assistance with applications and eligibility. |

## II. Licensing, Regulation, and Complaint Investigation

The regulation of long-term care facilities is divided between the Department of Public Health (DPH) for medical facilities and the Executive Office of Aging & Independence (AGE) for Assisted Living Residences.

### A. Nursing Homes and Rest Homes (DPH)

The **Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification** within the DPH is the primary regulatory body for nursing homes.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Key Responsibilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification** | **Website:** [https://www.mass.gov/orgs/division-of-health-care-facility-licensure-and-certification](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/division-of-health-care-facility-licensure-and-certification) | Licensing and certification of nursing homes, hospitals, and other medical facilities. |
| **Licensing Phone** | **Phone:** (617) 753-8000 | General inquiries regarding facility licensure. |
| **Complaint Hotline** | **Phone:** (617) 753-8150 | **To file a complaint** regarding a nursing home, rest home, or other licensed health care facility. |
| **DPH Mailing Address (for written complaints)** | Division of Health Care Facility Licensure and Certification, 67 Forest St., Marlborough, MA 01752 | Submission of formal, written complaints. |

### B. Assisted Living Residences (AGE)

The **Executive Office of Aging & Independence (AGE)** is responsible for the certification and oversight of Assisted Living Residences (ALRs).

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Key Responsibilities |
