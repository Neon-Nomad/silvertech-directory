# Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Long-Term Care in Kansas

Navigating the long-term care landscape in Kansas requires direct access to the state's primary regulatory, advocacy, and service-providing agencies. The **Kansas Department for Aging and Disability Services (KDADS)**, the **KanCare** system, the **Office of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman**, and the **Kansas Office of Veterans Services (KOVS)** are the key authorities responsible for licensing facilities, administering Medicaid, protecting resident rights, and supporting veterans. This guide provides a verified, centralized directory of essential contact information for these critical state authorities, ensuring residents, family members, and caregivers can quickly connect with the right office for information, assistance, or to file a complaint.

## I. State Regulatory and Medicaid Authority

The Kansas Department for Aging and Disability Services (KDADS) is the central state agency overseeing the long-term care system, including facility licensing and the administration of the KanCare (Medicaid) program.

### Kansas Department for Aging and Disability Services (KDADS)

KDADS is responsible for licensing adult care homes, investigating abuse and neglect, and managing the state's Home and Community Based Services (HCBS) waivers, such as the Frail Elderly (FE) Waiver.

### KanCare (Kansas Medicaid Program)

KanCare is the state's managed care program for Medicaid. The KanCare Clearinghouse handles eligibility and enrollment, while the Managed Care Enrollment Center assists with MCO selection.

## II. Resident Advocacy and Rights

The Office of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman is the official state advocate for residents of adult care homes, providing confidential complaint resolution and education on resident rights.

### Office of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman

The Ombudsman is an independent advocate for residents in nursing homes, assisted living, and other adult care homes.

## III. Veterans Services and Benefits

The Kansas Office of Veterans Services (KOVS) is dedicated to assisting veterans and their families in accessing state and federal benefits, including long-term care options at state veterans homes.
