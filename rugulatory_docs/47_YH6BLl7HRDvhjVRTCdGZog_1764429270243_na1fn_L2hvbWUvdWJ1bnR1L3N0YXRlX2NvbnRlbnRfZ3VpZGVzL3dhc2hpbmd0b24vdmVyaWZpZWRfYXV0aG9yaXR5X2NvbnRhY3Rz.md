# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Washington State

Navigating the long-term care system in Washington State requires interaction with multiple state agencies, each responsible for a different aspect of regulation, funding, and advocacy. This document provides a consolidated and verified list of the most critical government and non-profit authority contacts for Medicaid, licensing, complaints, and veterans' benefits.

## Detailed Agency Contact Information

### 1. Washington State Health Care Authority (HCA) - Apple Health (Medicaid)

The HCA is the primary agency for Washington's Medicaid program, known as Apple Health. They manage eligibility, enrollment, and the overall structure of the program, including the COPES Waiver for long-term care.

*   **Agency Name:** Washington State Health Care Authority (HCA)
*   **Primary Phone:** 1-800-562-3022
*   **Website:** [https://www.hca.wa.gov/](https://www.hca.wa.gov/)
*   **Key Program:** Apple Health (Medicaid)

### 2. Washington State Department of Health (DOH) - Nursing Home Licensing

The DOH's Health Systems Quality Assurance (HSQA) Division licenses and inspects all skilled nursing facilities (nursing homes) in the state. They are the regulatory authority for these facilities.

*   **Agency Name:** Washington State Department of Health (DOH)
*   **Division:** Health Systems Quality Assurance Division, Facilities Licensing
*   **Primary Phone:** 360-236-4700
*   **Licensing Website:** [https://doh.wa.gov/public-health-provider-resources/healthcare-professions-and-facilities/facilities-licensing](https://doh.wa.gov/public-health-provider-resources/healthcare-professions-and-facilities/facilities-licensing)
*   **Complaint Intake:** 1-800-633-6828

### 3. Washington State Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS)

DSHS manages licensing for residential care settings and operates the state's Adult Protective Services (APS) for vulnerable adults.

*   **Agency Name:** Washington State Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS)
*   **Residential Care Services (RCS) Complaint Hotline (ALF/AFH):** 1-800-562-6078
*   **Adult Protective Services (APS) Hotline:** 1-866-363-4276 (1-866-END-HARM)
*   **Website:** [https://www.dshs.wa.gov/](https://www.dshs.wa.gov/)

### 4. Washington State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)

The LTCOP is an independent advocacy program that investigates and resolves complaints made by or on behalf of residents of nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and adult family homes.

*   **Program Name:** Washington State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program
*   **Administered by:** Multi-Service Center (MSC)
*   **Statewide Phone:** 833-727-8900
*   **Website:** [https://www.waombudsman.org/](https://www.waombudsman.org/)

### 5. Washington State Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA)

The WDVA provides a wide range of state benefits, including the operation of the four State Veterans Homes and various financial assistance programs.

*   **Agency Name:** Washington State Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA)
*   **Primary Phone:** 1-800-562-2308
*   **Website:** [https://www.dva.wa.gov/](https://www.dva.wa.gov/)
*   **Email:** benefits@dva.wa.gov

***

## Important Note on Reporting

When reporting a complaint, always be prepared to provide the full name and address of the facility, the name of the resident, and specific details, including dates and times, of the incident(s). While you can remain anonymous when filing a complaint, providing contact information allows the investigator to follow up for clarification, which can strengthen the investigation.

For immediate danger or life-threatening situations, always call **9-1-1** first.
