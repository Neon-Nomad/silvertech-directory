# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care and Elder Services in Maine: A Comprehensive Resource Guide

This document provides a comprehensive and verified list of essential government agencies and non-profit organizations in Maine that oversee, regulate, and provide services for long-term care consumers, older adults, and veterans. These contacts are the primary resources for information, assistance, and filing complaints. The information is organized by the agency's primary function to help consumers quickly identify the correct resource.

## I. State Health and Human Services Agencies

The **Maine Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)** is the largest state agency and the umbrella organization for most state-level long-term care programs, including Medicaid (MaineCare), licensing, and protective services.

### A. DHHS Central Administration and Key Offices

| Agency/Program | Role and Scope of Services | Contact Information | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)** | The administrative office responsible for the overall health and welfare of Maine's citizens. It oversees all major state health, social, and long-term care programs. DHHS is structured into various offices and divisions to manage public assistance, child and family services, mental health, and substance abuse services. | Phone: (207) 287-3707 | [https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/) |
| **Office of MaineCare Services (OMS)** | **Administers MaineCare (Medicaid)**, the state's health care program for low-income individuals, families, and people with disabilities. This office manages eligibility, covered services, and the **Home and Community Benefits for the Elderly and Adults with Disabilities (Section 19)** waiver, which is crucial for assisted living and home care funding. OMS is the definitive source for all MaineCare policy and benefit questions. | Phone: (800) 977-6740 | [https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oms](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oms) |
| **Office of Aging and Disability Services (OADS)** | Oversees a wide range of services and supports for older adults and people with disabilities. OADS manages programs like the Area Agencies on Aging, home-based care services, and is the administrative home for Adult Protective Services. They are the central point for non-medical long-term care planning and resource connection. | Phone: (207) 287-9200 or (800) 262-2232 | [https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oads](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oads) |

### B. Regulatory and Protective Services

| Agency/Program | Role and Scope of Services | Contact Information | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Division of Licensing and Certification (DLC)** | The state's primary regulatory authority for long-term care facilities. DLC **licenses and certifies** all nursing homes, assisted living facilities, and residential care facilities. Their core function is to conduct unannounced inspections (surveys) and **investigate complaints** alleging violations of state licensing rules and federal certification standards. DLC's findings are public record. | Phone: (207) 287-3707 | [https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/dlc](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/dlc) |
| **DLC Complaint Hotline** | A dedicated, toll-free line for reporting regulatory non-compliance in licensed facilities. This is the correct number to call for issues related to quality of care, staffing, sanitation, and resident rights violations that do not involve immediate abuse or neglect. Callers can remain anonymous. | Toll-Free: (800) 383-2441 | [https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/dlc/safety-reporting/file-a-complaint](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/dlc/safety-reporting/file-a-complaint) |
| **Adult Protective Services (APS)** | The agency responsible for receiving and investigating reports of **abuse, neglect, or exploitation** of vulnerable adults (age 18+). APS provides protective services to ensure the safety and well-being of the adult and refers cases of criminal activity to law enforcement. This is the 24/7 emergency line for immediate safety concerns. | 24/7 Hotline: (800) 624-8404 | [https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oads/get-support/aps](https://www.maine.gov/dhhs/oads/get-support/aps) |

## II. Advocacy and Legal Assistance

These independent organizations provide free and confidential advocacy and legal services, ensuring residents' rights are protected and their voices are heard.

| Agency/Program | Role and Scope of Services | Contact Information | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maine Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (MLTCOP)** | **Independent advocate** for residents of nursing homes, assisted living, residential care, and recipients of home care. MLTCOP investigates complaints related to resident rights, quality of life, and care issues, working to resolve them through mediation and negotiation. Services are free and confidential and are often the first step for non-regulatory complaints. | Toll-Free: (800) 499-0229 | [https://www.maineombudsman.org/](https://www.maineombudsman.org/) |
| **Legal Services for Maine Elders (LSE)** | Provides **free legal advice and representation** to Mainers age 60 and older on civil legal matters that affect their basic needs, such as healthcare benefits (Medicare/MaineCare), housing, financial exploitation, and advance planning documents. LSE prioritizes cases with the greatest social and economic need. | Toll-Free: (800) 750-5353 | [https://mainelse.org/](https://mainelse.org/) |
| **Disability Rights Maine (DRM)** | The federally authorized **Protection and Advocacy (P&A)** system for Maine. DRM provides legal support and advocacy for people with disabilities, including those in long-term care settings, focusing on issues of abuse, neglect, and access to services in the least restrictive environment. They are a key resource for systemic change and individual rights protection. | Phone: (800) 452-1948 | [https://drme.org/](https://drme.org/) |

## III. Veterans' Services

The Maine Bureau of Veterans' Services and the Maine Veterans' Homes are the primary resources for veterans' benefits and care.

| Agency/Program | Role and Scope of Services | Contact Information | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Maine Bureau of Veterans' Services (MBVS)** | The state agency dedicated to assisting veterans, service members, and their families in obtaining all federal and state benefits. MBVS employs **Veterans Service Officers (VSOs)** who provide free assistance with VA claims, appeals, and information on state benefits like tax exemptions and educational waivers. | Phone: (207) 287-7020 | [https://www.maine.gov/veterans/](https://www.maine.gov/veterans/) |
| **Maine Veterans' Homes (MVH) Central Office** | Oversees the six state-operated veterans' homes that provide skilled nursing care, long-term care, and specialized dementia care to eligible veterans, spouses, and Gold Star Parents. The central office handles admissions and general inquiries for all six locations. | Toll-Free: (800) 278-9494 | [https://mainevets.org/](https://mainevets.org/) |
| **VA Medical Center (Togus)** | The primary federal VA health care facility in Maine, providing comprehensive medical, surgical, and mental health services to veterans. Togus also manages several Community-Based Outpatient Clinics (CBOCs) throughout the state. | Phone: (877) 424-8387 | [https://www.va.gov/maine-health-care/](https://www.va.gov/maine-health-care/) |
| **VA Regional Benefit Office** | The federal office responsible for administering non-medical benefits, such as disability compensation, pension, education, and home loan guarantees. This office is the point of contact for all federal VA benefits claims. | Phone: (800) 827-1000 | [https://www.va.gov/](https://www.va.gov/) |

## IV. Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs)

Maine's AAAs are non-profit organizations that serve as the local planning and service delivery system for older adults. They are a critical first point of contact for information and access to community-based services.

| Agency | Counties Served | Role and Scope of Services | Contact Information | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aroostook Agency on Aging** | Aroostook | Provides information, assistance, and services such as Meals on Wheels, transportation, and caregiver support to older adults in Aroostook County. They are the local resource for aging and disability options counseling. | Phone: (800) 439-1789 | [https://aroostookaging.org/](https://aroostookaging.org/) |
| **Eastern Area Agency on Aging** | Hancock, Penobscot, Piscataquis, Washington | Offers a comprehensive range of services, including Medicare counseling (SHIP), long-term care options counseling, and health promotion programs for the eastern region of Maine. They also manage the local Meals on Wheels program. | Phone: (800) 432-7812 | [https://eaaa.org/](https://eaaa.org/) |
| **Spectrum Generations** | Kennebec, Knox, Lincoln, Sagadahoc, Somerset, Waldo | Operates community centers and provides services such as Meals on Wheels, aging and disability resource counseling, and caregiver support across central Maine. They focus on promoting independence and well-being. | Phone: (800) 639-1553 | [https://www.spectrumgenerations.org/](https://www.spectrumgenerations.org/) |
| **Southern Maine Agency on Aging** | Cumberland, York | Serves the most populous region of Maine, offering a wide array of services including case management, health insurance counseling, and evidence-based health programs. They are a key resource for seniors in the southern part of the state. | Phone: (800) 582-7277 | [https://www.smaaa.org/](https://www.smaaa.org/) |
