# Navigating the Complaint Process for Long-Term Care in Georgia

The State of Georgia provides multiple, distinct avenues for residents, family members, and concerned citizens to report issues, file complaints, and seek resolution regarding the quality of care and services in long-term care facilities. Understanding which agency to contact for a specific type of complaint is the first and most crucial step in the process. The primary regulatory and investigative bodies are the **Healthcare Facility Regulation Division (HFRD)**, **Adult Protective Services (APS)**, and the **Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman (OSLTCO)**.

## 1. Regulatory Complaints: Healthcare Facility Regulation Division (HFRD)

The **Healthcare Facility Regulation Division (HFRD)** of the Georgia Department of Community Health (DCH) is the state's licensing and regulatory authority. HFRD is the correct agency to contact for complaints related to violations of state licensing standards or federal certification requirements (Medicare/Medicaid) [1].

### Scope of HFRD Complaints

HFRD investigates complaints that allege a facility is not meeting the minimum standards for health, safety, and quality of care. This includes, but is not limited to:

*   **Inadequate Care:** Failure to provide necessary medical, nursing, or personal care services.
*   **Environmental Hazards:** Unsafe or unsanitary conditions in the facility (e.g., fire safety violations, poor infection control).
*   **Medication Errors:** Repeated or serious errors in the administration of medication.
*   **Staffing Issues:** Insufficient staffing levels or unqualified personnel.
*   **Resident Rights Violations:** Violations of a resident's rights, such as involuntary discharge or improper use of restraints.

### How to File a Complaint with HFRD

HFRD maintains a dedicated Complaint Intake Unit to receive and process reports.

| HFRD Complaint Contact Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agency** | Healthcare Facility Regulation Division (HFRD) |
| **Toll-Free Complaint Hotline** | (800) 878-6442 [2] |
| **Direct Phone** | (404) 657-5726 or (404) 657-5728 |
| **Online Complaint Form** | [File a Complaint online here](https://dch.georgia.gov/divisionsoffices/facility-licensure/hfrd-file-complaint) [1] |
| **Fax** | (404) 657-8935 |

**The Investigation Process:**

1.  **Triage and Prioritization:** Upon receipt, the complaint is triaged based on the severity of the alleged violation. Complaints alleging immediate jeopardy to a resident's health or safety are prioritized for an on-site investigation within 24 to 48 hours.
2.  **On-Site Investigation:** HFRD surveyors conduct an unannounced visit to the facility. They interview residents, staff, and family members, observe care, and review medical records and facility policies.
3.  **Findings and Enforcement:** If the investigation substantiates the complaint, the facility is cited for the specific regulatory violations. The facility must then submit a **Plan of Correction (POC)** and is subject to follow-up surveys and potential enforcement actions, which can include fines or license sanctions.
4.  **Confidentiality:** The identity of the complainant is kept confidential and is not disclosed to the facility.

## 2. Abuse, Neglect, and Exploitation: Adult Protective Services (APS)

For reports of **abuse, neglect, or exploitation** of older persons (age 65 and older) or adults with disabilities (age 18 and older) who **do not reside in a long-term care facility**, the correct agency is **Adult Protective Services (APS)**, a program of the Georgia Department of Human Services (DHS) [3].

**Note:** While HFRD investigates abuse complaints in licensed facilities, APS is the primary investigative body for community-based settings and for self-neglect.

### What to Report to APS

APS investigates reports of:
*   **Physical Abuse:** Inflicting physical pain or injury.
*   **Emotional Abuse:** Inflicting mental pain, anguish, or distress.
*   **Neglect:** The failure of a caregiver to provide necessary care, or self-neglect where the adult is unable to provide for their own needs.
*   **Exploitation:** The illegal or improper use of an adult's funds, property, or resources.

### How to Report to APS

Reports to APS can be made 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

| APS Contact Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Agency** | Adult Protective Services (APS) |
| **Statewide APS Intake Hotline** | (866) 55AGING or (866) 552-4464 (Option 3) [4] |
| **Online Reporting** | Georgia Online APS Reporting System (if available) |

**The APS Investigation:**

APS investigators determine if the adult is in need of protective services. If the report is substantiated, APS works with the adult to develop a plan for protection, which may include connecting them with community resources, legal services, or other support.

## 3. Advocacy and Informal Resolution: Long-Term Care Ombudsman

The **Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman (OSLTCO)** is the best resource for residents and families seeking an independent advocate to resolve quality of life issues, disputes with facility staff, or concerns about resident rights [5].

### Scope of Ombudsman Complaints

The Ombudsman program focuses on informal resolution and advocacy. Complaints handled by the Ombudsman include:
*   **Resident Rights:** Violations of the right to privacy, the right to choose, or the right to voice grievances.
*   **Quality of Life:** Issues related to food, activities, laundry, or facility environment.
*   **Communication:** Disputes or breakdowns in communication between the resident, family, and facility staff.
*   **Transfer/Discharge:** Concerns about involuntary transfer or discharge from a facility.

### How to Contact the Ombudsman

The Ombudsman program is confidential and resident-directed.

| Ombudsman Contact Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Program** | Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman |
| **Toll-Free Phone** | (866) 552-4464 (Georgia Aging and Disability Resource Connection) [5] |
| **Website** | [https://www.georgiaombudsman.org/](https://www.georgiaombudsman.org/) |

**The Ombudsman Process:**

The Ombudsman's strength lies in its ability to mediate and negotiate. They cannot issue citations, but they can bring the weight of the law and regulation to bear in discussions with facility management, often resolving issues quickly and to the resident's satisfaction.

## 4. Managed Care and Medicaid Appeals

For complaints related to Medicaid services, managed care organizations (CMOs), or denials of coverage, a separate process is required.

### Managed Care Grievances and Appeals

Most Georgia Medicaid members are enrolled in a CMO (e.g., Amerigroup, CareSource, Peach State Health Plan). If a member is unhappy with the quality of care, a provider, or a service, they must first follow the CMO's internal **Grievance** process.

*   **Grievance:** A complaint about the quality of care, services, or the behavior of a provider. Members should contact their CMO's Member Services line to file a grievance.
*   **Appeal:** A request to review a decision by the CMO to deny, reduce, or terminate a service or benefit. Members have the right to appeal this decision.

### Fair Hearing (State Appeal)

If a member is dissatisfied with the outcome of the CMO's internal appeal, they have the right to request a **State Fair Hearing** [6]. This is an administrative hearing conducted by an independent state entity, where the member can present their case against the CMO's decision.

| :--- | :--- |
| **Initial Step** | Contact your specific Managed Care Organization (CMO) Member Services |
| **Next Step** | Request a State Fair Hearing if the CMO appeal is denied |
| **General Medicaid Inquiries** | Georgia Department of Community Health (DCH) |

## Summary of Complaint Pathways

The following table summarizes the appropriate agency to contact based on the nature of the complaint:

| Nature of Complaint | Appropriate Agency | Contact Method | Primary Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Regulatory Violation** (Health, Safety, Staffing, Licensing) | HFRD (Healthcare Facility Regulation Division) | Toll-Free Hotline: (800) 878-6442 | Investigation, Citation, Enforcement |
| **Abuse, Neglect, Exploitation** (Community-based) | Adult Protective Services (APS) | Hotline: (866) 552-4464 (Option 3) | Investigation, Protection, Intervention |
| **Resident Rights, Quality of Life, Disputes** | OSLTCO (Long-Term Care Ombudsman) | Toll-Free Hotline: (866) 552-4464 | Advocacy, Mediation, Informal Resolution |
| **Medicaid Service Denial or Reduction** | Managed Care Organization (CMO) / State Fair Hearing | CMO Member Services / State Appeal Request | Service Approval, Benefit Restoration |

By utilizing the correct channel, Georgians can ensure their concerns are directed to the authority with the power to investigate and resolve the issue, thereby upholding the standards of care and protecting the rights of vulnerable adults.
