# Wyoming Healthcare Facility Complaint Process: How to Report Concerns to the Department of Health

## The Central Authority for Complaints: Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS)

In Wyoming, the **Healthcare Licensing and Surveys (HLS)** division of the **Wyoming Department of Health** is the designated state agency responsible for receiving, investigating, and resolving complaints against licensed healthcare facilities. HLS plays a critical role in ensuring that all facilities comply with state and federal regulations concerning patient safety, quality of care, and resident rights.

The process for filing a complaint is designed to be accessible to residents, family members, facility staff, and the general public. All complaints are taken seriously and are handled with confidentiality.

## Facilities and Issues Covered by HLS

HLS has jurisdiction over a wide array of licensed healthcare facilities in Wyoming. Complaints related to the following types of facilities and issues should be directed to HLS:

### Covered Facilities
*   **Nursing Care Facilities (Nursing Homes)**
*   **Assisted Living Facilities**
*   **Hospitals** (including Critical Access Hospitals)
*   **Home Health Agencies**
*   **Hospice Programs**
*   **Adult Day Care Facilities**
*   **Boarding Homes**

### Types of Reportable Complaints
Complaints can cover any issue that may violate state or federal regulations, including:
*   **Quality of Care:** Inadequate medical care, medication errors, poor wound care, or failure to follow a care plan.
*   **Resident Rights Violations:** Infringement on privacy, lack of dignity, or unauthorized use of restraints.
*   **Abuse, Neglect, or Exploitation:** Physical, emotional, or sexual abuse; financial exploitation; or neglect leading to harm or injury.
*   **Environmental Safety:** Unsafe conditions, poor sanitation, fire hazards, or inadequate maintenance.
*   **Dietary Concerns:** Insufficient nutrition, unappetizing food, or failure to accommodate special dietary needs.

## The Complaint Filing Process

Filing a complaint with HLS can be done in several ways, and complainants have the option to remain anonymous.

### 1. Filing by Phone (Hotline)
The most direct way to file a complaint, especially for urgent matters, is by calling the toll-free complaint hotline: **1-800-548-1367**. Trained staff will take down the details of the complaint, including:
*   The name and location of the facility.
*   The name of the resident (if applicable).
*   A detailed description of the incident, including dates and times.
*   The names of any witnesses.

### 2. Filing by Mail or Fax
Complainants can download and complete the official **Healthcare Facility Complaint Form** from the Wyoming Department of Health website. The completed form can then be mailed or faxed to the HLS office in Cheyenne. Using the form ensures that all necessary information is provided to initiate a thorough investigation.

### 3. Anonymity and Confidentiality
HLS accepts complaints from anonymous sources. If a complainant chooses to provide their name, their identity will be kept confidential and will not be disclosed to the facility without their express written consent. This protection is critical to encourage reporting without fear of retaliation.

## The Complaint Investigation and Resolution

Once a complaint is received, HLS follows a structured process to investigate the allegations:

1.  **Triage and Prioritization:** Complaints are assessed for severity and immediacy. Allegations of immediate jeopardy to a resident's health or safety are prioritized and typically investigated within two working days.
2.  **On-Site Investigation:** HLS surveyors conduct an unannounced, on-site investigation at the facility. This involves interviewing residents, staff, and family members; observing care; and reviewing medical records and facility documents.
3.  **Findings and Citations:** If the investigation confirms that a violation of state or federal regulations occurred, the facility is cited for the deficiency.
4.  **Plan of Correction (POC):** The facility is required to submit a Plan of Correction detailing the steps they will take to remedy the deficiency and prevent recurrence. HLS monitors the facility to ensure the POC is implemented effectively.
5.  **Notification:** The complainant is notified of the outcome of the investigation, though specific details may be limited to protect the privacy of the resident involved.

## HLS vs. The Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

It is important to understand the distinction between the HLS complaint process and the role of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP).

| Feature | HLS (Healthcare Licensing and Surveys) | LTCOP (Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Role** | Regulatory enforcement and investigation of violations. | Advocacy, mediation, and protection of resident rights. |
| **Focus** | Compliance with state and federal laws and regulations. | Quality of life, resident choice, and resolution of individual concerns. |
| **Outcome** | Citations, fines, and mandated Plans of Correction. | Resolution of the issue, education, and systemic change advocacy. |
| **Authority** | Legal authority to enforce regulations and impose sanctions. | Authority to investigate and mediate, but no regulatory enforcement power. |

While HLS is the enforcement arm, the Ombudsman is the advocate. Residents and families are encouraged to contact **both** the Ombudsman for advocacy and the HLS for formal complaints regarding regulatory violations.

## Conclusion

The Wyoming Department of Health's HLS division provides a robust and confidential system for reporting concerns about healthcare facilities. By utilizing the toll-free hotline or the official complaint form, citizens can play an active role in ensuring that long-term care facilities in Wyoming maintain the highest standards of safety and quality.
