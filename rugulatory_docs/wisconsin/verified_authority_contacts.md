# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care and Senior Services in Wisconsin

Accessing reliable information and support for long-term care, senior services, and veterans benefits in Wisconsin requires navigating a network of state agencies and independent advocacy organizations. This document serves as a comprehensive, verified directory of the essential authority contacts, providing the necessary phone numbers, websites, and mailing addresses for the most critical state-level programs. These contacts are the official gateways to services, regulatory oversight, and advocacy for Wisconsin residents, particularly those who are elderly, have disabilities, or are veterans.

The information compiled here is drawn directly from the Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS), the State of Wisconsin Board on Aging and Long Term Care (BOALTC), the Wisconsin Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA), and key advocacy partners. Utilizing these verified contacts ensures that individuals, families, and caregivers can connect directly with the authoritative source for their specific needs, whether it involves applying for Medicaid, reporting a complaint, or seeking veterans' assistance.

---

## 1. Medicaid and Long-Term Care Programs: Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS)

The **Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS)** is the central state agency responsible for administering the state's Medicaid program, branded as **ForwardHealth**, and overseeing the primary long-term care programs, **Family Care** and **IRIS (Include, Respect, I Self-Direct)**. The contacts below are for general inquiries, application assistance, and information regarding eligibility for these vital programs.

### DHS Main Office and ForwardHealth Contacts

| Contact Category | Agency/Program | Phone Number | Website/Address |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State Agency Main Office** | Wisconsin Department of Health Services (DHS) | (608) 266-1865 | 201 E. Washington Ave., Madison, WI 53703 [1] |
| **Medicaid Program (ForwardHealth)** | Wisconsin Medicaid Member Services | (800) 362-3002 | [https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/medicaid/index.htm](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/medicaid/index.htm) [2] |
| **Online Application Portal** | ACCESS Wisconsin | (800) 362-3002 | [https://access.wi.gov/](https://access.wi.gov/) [3] |
| **IRIS Program Information** | IRIS Management Section | (888) 515-4747 (IRIS Call Center) | [https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/iris/index.htm](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/iris/index.htm) [4] |

### Role of the DHS in Long-Term Care

The DHS is the foundational authority for long-term care in Wisconsin. The **Medicaid Member Services** line is the primary contact for current and prospective members of ForwardHealth, offering assistance with eligibility questions, benefit coverage, and general program navigation. The **ACCESS Wisconsin** portal is the fastest and most efficient way to apply for Medicaid and other state benefits.

The DHS also oversees the **Aging and Disability Resource Centers (ADRCs)**, which serve as the single point of entry for long-term care. While the ADRC contact varies by county, the DHS website provides the necessary resources to locate the local ADRC, which is essential for initiating the **Long-Term Care Functional Screen (LTCFS)** and receiving options counseling [5]. The ADRC is the first step for anyone seeking to enroll in Family Care or IRIS.

## 2. Licensing, Regulation, and Quality Assurance: Division of Quality Assurance (DQA)

The **Division of Quality Assurance (DQA)**, a division within the DHS, is the state's regulatory body responsible for licensing, certifying, and inspecting all long-term care facilities, including nursing homes and assisted living facilities (CBRFs and RCACs). These contacts are for inquiries related to facility compliance, licensing requirements, and general oversight.

### DQA Licensing and Oversight Contacts

| Contact Category | Agency/Program | Phone Number | Website/Address |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Licensing Authority** | DHS, Bureau of Nursing Home Resident Care (DQA) | (608) 266-2966 | [https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/service/nursing-home-licensing-bureau-nursing-home-resident-care](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/service/nursing-home-licensing-bureau-nursing-home-resident-care) [6] |
| **Assisted Living Oversight** | DHS, Bureau of Assisted Living (DQA) | (608) 266-8482 | [https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/dqa/index.htm](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/dqa/index.htm) [7] |
| **Complaint Hotline** | DQA Complaint Intake (24/7 for emergencies) | **(800) 642-6552** | [https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/guide/complaints.htm](https://www.dhs.wisconsin.gov/guide/complaints.htm) [8] |

### The DQA's Regulatory Mandate

The DQA is the enforcement arm of the state for long-term care quality. The **Licensing Authority** contact is primarily for facility operators and those seeking information on regulatory standards (DHS 132 for Nursing Homes, DHS 83 for CBRFs, DHS 89 for RCACs).

The **DQA Complaint Hotline** is the most critical contact for the public. This toll-free number is the direct line for reporting suspected abuse, neglect, financial exploitation, or any violation of state or federal regulations in a licensed facility. All complaints are confidential, and the DQA is legally mandated to investigate all reports, prioritizing those that pose an immediate threat to resident health and safety [8]. The DQA's website also provides the link to the **online Complaint Intake Survey (F-00607)**, which is recommended for non-emergency reports.

## 3. Ombudsman and Advocacy Services

Wisconsin’s advocacy system is divided to provide specialized support for different populations. The **Ombudsman Program** provides independent, confidential advocacy for long-term care consumers, protecting their rights and resolving complaints.

### A. Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (BOALTC)

The **State of Wisconsin Board on Aging and Long Term Care (BOALTC)** administers the Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program for **individuals aged 60 and older** in all long-term care facilities.

| Contact Category | Agency/Program | Phone Number | Website/Address |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ombudsman Program (Age 60+)** | BOALTC, Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program | **(800) 815-0015** | [https://longtermcare.wi.gov/Pages/Ombudsman.aspx](https://longtermcare.wi.gov/Pages/Ombudsman.aspx) [9] |
| **BOALTC Main Office** | State of Wisconsin Board on Aging and Long Term Care | (608) 246-7013 (General) | 1402 Pankratz Street, Suite 111, Madison, WI 53704 [10] |
| **Medigap Helpline** | BOALTC Medigap Helpline | (800) 242-1060 | [https://longtermcare.wi.gov/Pages/Medigap.aspx](https://longtermcare.wi.gov/Pages/Medigap.aspx) [11] |

The BOALTC Ombudsman is the primary advocate for elders in nursing homes, CBRFs, and RCACs, addressing issues related to resident rights, quality of life, and facility practices. The **Medigap Helpline** is a separate, critical service provided by BOALTC, offering free, confidential counseling on Medicare, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), and Medicare Advantage plans.

### B. Family Care and IRIS Ombudsman Program (DRW)

**Disability Rights Wisconsin (DRW)**, the state's Protection and Advocacy (P&A) system, administers the ombudsman program for **adults with disabilities aged 18-59** who are applicants or members of the Medicaid managed care programs (Family Care and IRIS).

| Contact Category | Agency/Program | Phone Number | Website/Address |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ombudsman Program (Age 18-59)** | Disability Rights Wisconsin (DRW) | **(800) 928-8778** | [https://disabilityrightswi.org/](https://disabilityrightswi.org/) [12] |
| **DRW TTY** | Disability Rights Wisconsin | (888) 758-6049 | [https://disabilityrightswi.org/contact-us/](https://disabilityrightswi.org/contact-us/) [13] |

The DRW Ombudsman specializes in managed care issues, helping consumers navigate service denials, grievances, and appeals within the Family Care and IRIS programs. This contact is essential for younger adults with disabilities who are seeking to access or maintain their long-term care waiver services.

## 4. Veterans Benefits and Services: Wisconsin Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA)

The **Wisconsin Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA)** is the state agency dedicated to providing comprehensive benefits and services to Wisconsin veterans, their families, and their dependents.

### WDVA Key Contacts

| Contact Category | Agency/Program | Phone Number | Website/Address |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Veterans Affairs Agency** | Wisconsin Department of Veterans Affairs (WDVA) | **(800) 947-8387** (1-800-WIS-VETS) | [http://dva.wi.gov/](http://dva.wi.gov/) [14] |
| **WDVA Mailing Address** | WDVA Main Office | (608) 266-1311 (General) | 2135 Rimrock Road, PO Box 7843, Madison, WI 53707-7843 [15] |
| **Veterans Homes Information** | Wisconsin Veterans Homes | (800) 947-8387 | [https://dva.wi.gov/services/wisconsin-veterans-homes/](https://dva.wi.gov/services/wisconsin-veterans-homes/) [16] |
| **Wisconsin GI Bill** | WDVA Education Benefits | (800) 947-8387 | [https://dva.wi.gov/benefits/education/wi-gi-bill/](https://dva.wi.gov/benefits/education/wi-gi-bill/) [17] |

### The Importance of the WDVA

The WDVA is the authoritative source for all state-level veterans' benefits, including the generous **Veterans and Surviving Spouses Property Tax Credit** and the **Wisconsin GI Bill** (tuition remission). The WDVA also oversees the three **Wisconsin Veterans Homes** (King, Union Grove, and Chippewa Falls), which provide long-term care.

Veterans are strongly advised to contact the WDVA or their local **County Veterans Service Officer (CVSO)** to ensure they are maximizing all state and federal benefits they have earned. The WDVA toll-free number is the central point of contact for all these programs.

## Conclusion: Utilizing Verified Authority Contacts

Connecting with the correct authority is the essential first step in resolving issues, accessing benefits, and ensuring quality care in Wisconsin. The state's system is structured to provide clear pathways for consumers:

*   **For Eligibility and Enrollment:** Start with the **DHS Medicaid Member Services** and the **local ADRC** to determine eligibility for Family Care or IRIS.
*   **For Regulatory Issues:** Use the **DQA Complaint Hotline** to report serious concerns about facility compliance and safety.
*   **For Rights and Advocacy:** Contact the appropriate **Ombudsman Program** (BOALTC for 60+, DRW for 18-59) for confidential assistance with resident rights and service disputes.
*   **For Veterans:** The **WDVA** is the single point of contact for all state-specific veterans' benefits.

By relying on these verified authority contacts, Wisconsin residents and their families can confidently navigate the complex landscape of long-term care, ensuring they receive the benefits and protections they are entitled to under state and federal law.
