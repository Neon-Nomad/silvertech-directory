# Tennessee Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Long-Term Care and Senior Services

Navigating the landscape of long-term care, senior services, and veterans benefits in Tennessee requires access to accurate and reliable contact information for the state's key governmental and advocacy agencies. This guide provides a comprehensive, verified directory of the most critical authority contacts, ensuring residents, family members, and caregivers can quickly connect with the correct state division for assistance, information, and complaint resolution. All contact details are sourced directly from official Tennessee state government websites.
