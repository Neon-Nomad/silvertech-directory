# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Hawaii

This document provides a consolidated list of essential contact information for the primary state agencies and programs responsible for regulating, overseeing, and advocating for long-term care services in Hawaii. These contacts are crucial resources for residents, family members, and caregivers seeking information, filing complaints, or applying for benefits.

## 1. Medicaid and Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS)

The Hawaii Medicaid program, known as QUEST Integration, is administered by the Med-QUEST Division of the Department of Human Services. This agency handles eligibility, enrollment, and oversight of the managed care organizations (MCOs) that provide both acute and long-term care services.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Name** | Department of Human Services Med-QUEST Division | Administers the QUEST Integration Medicaid program. |
| **Program Name** | QUEST Integration | Hawaii's comprehensive Medicaid program. |
| **Website** | [https://medquest.hawaii.gov/](https://medquest.hawaii.gov/) | Official website for the Med-QUEST Division. |
| **General Phone** | (800) 316-8005 | Toll-free number for general inquiries and application assistance. |
| **Grievance/Appeals** | (808) 692-8094 | Phone number for the Med-QUEST Division's Grievance and Appeals office (for Fair Hearings). |

## 2. Licensing, Certification, and Facility Complaints

The Office of Health Care Assurance (OHCA) is the state authority responsible for licensing long-term care facilities, conducting inspections, and investigating complaints related to regulatory violations, quality of care, and safety.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Name** | Office of Health Care Assurance (OHCA), DOH | State licensing and federal certification authority for healthcare facilities. |
| **Website** | [https://health.hawaii.gov/ohca/](https://health.hawaii.gov/ohca/) | Official website for the Office of Health Care Assurance. |
| **Licensing/General Phone** | (808) 692-7400 | General phone number for the State Licensing Section. |
| **Complaint Hotline** | (808) 692-7420 | Dedicated line for filing complaints against licensed facilities (Medicare Section). |

| **Complaint Fax** | (808) 692-7414 | Fax number for submitting the official complaint form. |

## 3. Ombudsman and Resident Advocacy

The Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP) is an independent advocate for residents of nursing homes, adult residential care homes, and assisted living facilities. The service is confidential and focuses on resolving complaints related to resident rights and quality of life.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agency Name** | Executive Office on Aging (EOA), DOH | Houses the Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program. |
| **Program Name** | Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program | Resident advocacy and complaint resolution. |
| **Toll-Free Phone** | (888) 229-2231 | Toll-free number for the Long-Term Care Ombudsman. |
| **Website** | [https://www.hi-ltc-ombudsman.org/](https://www.hi-ltc-ombudsman.org/) | Official website for the LTCOP. |

## 5. Adult Protective Services (APS)

For immediate concerns regarding suspected abuse, neglect, or financial exploitation of a vulnerable adult, contact Adult Protective Services (APS), which provides crisis intervention and investigation services.

| **Agency Name** | Adult Protective Services (APS), DHS | Investigates abuse, neglect, and exploitation of vulnerable adults. |
| **APS Reporting Line** | (808) 832-5115 | Statewide line for reporting suspected abuse. |
| **Website** | [https://humanservices.hawaii.gov/ssd/home/adult-services/](https://humanservices.hawaii.gov/ssd/home/adult-services/) | Department of Human Services Adult Services website. |
