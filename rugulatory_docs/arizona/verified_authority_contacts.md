## Detailed Agency Profiles

### 1. Arizona Health Care Cost Containment System (AHCCCS)

AHCCCS is Arizona's Medicaid agency, operating under a unique managed care model. It is the single most important resource for low-income seniors and individuals with disabilities seeking financial assistance for healthcare and long-term care services.

#### Primary Role: Medicaid Administration
AHCCCS administers the **Arizona Long Term Care System (ALTCS)**, which is the state's comprehensive program covering the costs of skilled nursing facility care, assisted living services, and Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) for those who meet medical and financial eligibility requirements [1].

**Key Services:**

*   Contracting with managed care organizations (Program Contractors) to deliver services.

### 2. Arizona Department of Health Services (ADHS)

The ADHS, through its Division of Licensing Services, is the state's regulatory body for all healthcare institutions, including long-term care facilities. Its function is to ensure that facilities meet minimum standards for health, safety, and quality of care.

#### Primary Role: Facility Licensing and Regulation
ADHS is responsible for issuing, renewing, and revoking licenses for nursing care institutions (nursing homes) and assisted living facilities. They conduct routine, unannounced inspections and investigate complaints related to licensing violations [2].

### 3. Complaint and Protective Services

Arizona utilizes a dual system for handling complaints, distinguishing between regulatory violations and criminal acts of mistreatment.

#### A. ADHS Complaint Hotline (Regulatory Complaints)
This is the primary contact for reporting concerns about the quality of care, sanitation, staffing, or any other violation of licensing standards within a facility.

#### B. Adult Protective Services (APS)
APS is responsible for investigating allegations of abuse, neglect, and exploitation of vulnerable adults, including those residing in long-term care facilities. **Any suspicion of physical, emotional, or sexual abuse, or financial exploitation, should be reported immediately to APS.**

### 4. Arizona State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)

The LTCOP is a free, confidential advocacy service dedicated to protecting the rights and welfare of residents in long-term care facilities. The Ombudsman acts as a mediator and advocate, working to resolve complaints from the resident's perspective.

#### Primary Role: Resident Advocacy
The Ombudsman's role is distinct from that of ADHS and APS. They do not issue citations or prosecute, but rather empower residents, investigate complaints related to resident rights, and work to improve the quality of life and care in facilities [3].

### 5. Arizona Department of Veterans' Services (DVS)

The DVS is the state agency that assists veterans, service members, and their families in accessing federal and state benefits, including long-term care options.

#### Primary Role: Veterans' Benefits and Care
The DVS operates the Arizona State Veterans Homes (ASVH), which provide skilled nursing care to eligible veterans, their spouses, and surviving spouses. They also manage a network of Veteran Benefits Counselors (VBCs) who provide free assistance with claims and applications [4].
