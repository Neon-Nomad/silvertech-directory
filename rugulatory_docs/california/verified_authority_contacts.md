# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in California

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care in California requires direct access to the state's primary regulatory, advocacy, and benefit-providing agencies. This document provides a consolidated, verified list of essential authority contacts for the state of California, covering Medicaid, licensing, consumer advocacy, and veterans' services.

## 1. Medicaid Programs: Medi-Cal

The **California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS)** administers Medi-Cal, the state's Medicaid program, which is the primary payer for long-term care services, including the Assisted Living Waiver (ALW) and skilled nursing facility care.

| Agency | Program | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS)** | Medi-Cal | [https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/medi-cal/Pages/default.aspx](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/medi-cal/Pages/default.aspx) | (800) 541-5555 |
| **Medi-Cal Assisted Living Waiver (ALW)** | DHCS Long-Term Care Division | [https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/ltc/Pages/AssistedLivingWaiver.aspx](https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/ltc/Pages/AssistedLivingWaiver.aspx) | Contact local Care Coordination Agency (CCA) for enrollment |

## 2. Licensing and Certification Authority

The **California Department of Public Health (CDPH)**, through its Licensing and Certification Program, is responsible for the regulatory oversight of skilled nursing facilities (SNFs) and certain health care professionals.

| **California Department of Public Health (CDPH)** | Licensing and Certification Program (L&C) | [https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CHCQ/LCP/Pages/LandCProgramHome.aspx](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CHCQ/LCP/Pages/LandCProgramHome.aspx) | (800) 236-9747 |
| **CDPH - Residential Care Facilities (RCFE)** | *Note: RCFEs (Assisted Living) are licensed by the Department of Social Services (CDSS).* | N/A | N/A |

## 3. Ombudsman and Advocacy

The **California Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** is the state's official advocate for residents in skilled nursing facilities and residential care facilities (assisted living).

| Agency | Program | Website | Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **California Department of Aging (CDA)** | Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program | [https://aging.ca.gov/Programs_and_Services/Long-Term_Care_Ombudsman/](https://aging.ca.gov/Programs_and_Services/Long-Term_Care_Ombudsman/) | **(800) 231-4024** (Statewide Crisis Line) |

## 4. Complaint Process

Complaints against long-term care facilities are handled by two separate state agencies, depending on the facility type.

| Agency | Facility Type | Complaint Hotline | Online Complaint Filing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **California Department of Public Health (CDPH)** | Skilled Nursing Facilities (SNFs) | **(800) 554-0354** | [Cal Health Find Online Complaint](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CHCQ/LCP/Pages/FileAComplaint.aspx) |
| **California Department of Social Services (CDSS)** | Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly (RCFEs) / Assisted Living | **(844) 538-8766** (LET-US-NO) | [CDSS Online Complaint System](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/community-care-licensing/file-a-complaint) |
