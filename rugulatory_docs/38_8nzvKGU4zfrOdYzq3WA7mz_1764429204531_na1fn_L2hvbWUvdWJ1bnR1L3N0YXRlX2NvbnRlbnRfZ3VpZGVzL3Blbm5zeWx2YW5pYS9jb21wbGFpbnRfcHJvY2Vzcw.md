# Pennsylvania Long-Term Care Complaint Process: A Guide to Reporting Facility Concerns

For residents, family members, and concerned citizens in Pennsylvania, understanding the proper channels for filing a complaint against a long-term care facility is essential for ensuring quality of care and resident safety. The complaint process is managed by two primary state agencies, depending on the type of facility: the **Department of Health (DOH)** for nursing homes, and the **Department of Human Services (DHS)** for Assisted Living Residences (ALRs) and Personal Care Homes (PCHs).

This comprehensive guide details the specific procedures, contact information, and best practices for submitting a formal complaint against a long-term care facility in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

## The Two-Track Complaint System

Pennsylvania's regulatory structure necessitates a two-track system for complaints, corresponding to the licensing authority of the facility.

| Facility Type | Licensing/Complaint Authority | Complaint Hotline | Online Complaint Form |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## Track 1: Complaints Against Nursing Homes (Department of Health)

The **Pennsylvania Department of Health (DOH)**, through its **Division of Nursing Care Facilities**, is responsible for investigating all complaints related to the care, treatment, and rights of residents in licensed nursing homes.

### Methods for Filing a Complaint

The DOH provides multiple confidential methods for filing a complaint, ensuring accessibility for all complainants.

#### 1. DOH Complaint Hotline (Recommended)

*   **Phone Number:** **1-800-254-5164**
*   **Availability:** The hotline is staffed by DOH professionals. Callers are encouraged to leave a detailed message if the call is not immediately answered, including the facility name, the specific issue, and contact information for a return call.
*   **Confidentiality:** The DOH is legally required to protect the identity of the complainant.

#### 2. Online Complaint Form

*   **URL:** [https://apps.health.pa.gov/dohforms/FacilityComplaint.aspx] [1]
*   **Process:** The online form allows for a structured submission of details, including the facility name, address, date of incident, and a description of the complaint.

#### 3. Email and Mail

For those who prefer written communication, complaints can be submitted via email or postal mail.

*   **Email:** `c-ncomplai@pa.gov`
*   **Mailing Address:**
    > Division of Nursing Care Facilities Director
    > Pennsylvania Department of Health
    > Division of Nursing Care Facilities
    > 2525 N. 7th Street, Suite 210
    > Harrisburg, PA 17120

#### 4. Fax

*   **Fax Number:** 717-772-2163

### The DOH Investigation Process

1.  **Triage:** The DOH reviews the complaint to determine its severity and whether it falls under DOH jurisdiction.
2.  **Investigation:** Complaints alleging immediate jeopardy to a resident's health or safety are investigated within **24 hours**. Other complaints are investigated within a specified timeframe. Investigations are typically unannounced.
3.  **Findings:** If the investigation confirms a violation of state or federal regulations, the DOH issues a Statement of Deficiencies to the facility.
4.  **Enforcement:** The facility must submit a Plan of Correction (POC). Failure to correct deficiencies can result in sanctions, including fines, denial of payment, or license revocation.

## Track 2: Complaints Against Assisted Living and Personal Care Homes (Department of Human Services)

The **Department of Human Services (DHS)**, through its **Bureau of Human Services Licensing (BHSL)**, is the authority for complaints concerning **Assisted Living Residences (ALRs)** and **Personal Care Homes (PCHs)**.

### Methods for Filing a Complaint

The DHS BHSL also offers multiple avenues for reporting concerns about ALRs and PCHs.

#### 1. DHS BHSL Complaint Hotline (Recommended)

*   **Phone Number:** **1-877-401-8835**
*   **Process:** This toll-free number connects callers directly to the BHSL to report concerns about PCHs and ALRs.

#### 2. Online Complaint Form

*   **URL:** [DHS BHSL Online Complaint Form] [2]
*   **Process:** The online form is the most efficient way to submit a detailed, written complaint to the BHSL.

#### 3. Email

*   **Email:** `ra-pwarlheadquarters@pa.gov` (This is the general BHSL headquarters email, which can be used for complaint submission.)

### The DHS BHSL Investigation Process

The BHSL's process is similar to the DOH's, focusing on compliance with **55 Pa. Code Chapter 2600 (PCH)** and **Chapter 2800 (ALR)**.

1.  **Complaint Intake:** The BHSL receives and logs the complaint.
2.  **Investigation:** Investigators conduct unannounced visits to the facility to interview residents, staff, and review records.
3.  **Resolution:** If violations are found, the BHSL issues a citation and requires the facility to submit a plan to correct the deficiencies.
4.  **Public Records:** The results of BHSL investigations are made public and can be accessed through the DHS Human Services Provider Directory Search Form [3].

## The Role of the Long-Term Care Ombudsman

Before or in parallel with filing a formal regulatory complaint, residents and families should utilize the **Pennsylvania Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program**. The Ombudsman acts as an advocate and mediator, often resolving issues more quickly and informally than a formal investigation.

### Ombudsman Contact Information

*   **Ombudsman Program Name:** Pennsylvania Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program
*   **Administering Agency:** Department of Aging
*   **State Office Phone:** **717-783-8975**
*   **State Office Email:** `LTC-Ombudsman@pa.gov`

### When to Contact the Ombudsman

*   **Before Filing:** To get advice on the best way to approach a problem or to mediate a dispute with the facility's management.
*   **During the Process:** If the complainant fears retaliation or needs an independent advocate to ensure their rights are respected during a DOH or DHS investigation.
*   **For Quality of Life Issues:** The Ombudsman is particularly effective at resolving non-regulatory issues related to dignity, respect, and quality of life that may not trigger a formal citation from a licensing agency.

## Reporting Elder Abuse and Neglect

Complaints involving suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of an older adult (age 60 and older) must be reported immediately to **Older Adult Protective Services (OAPS)**. This is a separate, critical function distinct from the facility licensing complaint process.

### Statewide Elder Abuse Hotline (24/7)

*   **Phone Number:** **1-800-490-8505**
*   **Purpose:** This 24-hour, toll-free hotline is the single point of contact for reporting suspected abuse, neglect, self-neglect, or financial exploitation of an older adult.
*   **Mandatory Reporting:** Pennsylvania has mandatory reporting laws, meaning certain professionals are legally required to report suspected abuse.

| Type of Complaint | Agency to Contact | Hotline Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nursing Home Care/Rights** | Department of Health (DOH) | **1-800-254-5164** |
| **PCH/ALR Care/Rights** | DHS Bureau of Human Services Licensing (BHSL) | **1-877-401-8835** |
| **Advocacy/Mediation** | Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program | **717-783-8975** |
| **Suspected Elder Abuse (24/7)** | Older Adult Protective Services (OAPS) | **1-800-490-8505** |
