# Verified Authority Contacts in New Hampshire: A Comprehensive Directory for Healthcare, Advocacy, and Veterans Services

Navigating the complex landscape of healthcare, long-term care, and veterans benefits in **New Hampshire** requires direct access to the correct state and federal agencies. The **Granite State** maintains a clear structure of governmental bodies responsible for administering Medicaid, licensing health facilities, protecting vulnerable adults, and supporting its veteran population. This comprehensive directory serves as a vital resource, consolidating the verified contact information for the key authorities, detailing their specific roles, and explaining when and how to engage with each office.

This guide is structured to ensure that residents, family members, and advocates can quickly identify the appropriate agency for their needs, whether it involves applying for benefits, reporting a complaint, or seeking advocacy.

## I. New Hampshire Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)

The **New Hampshire Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS)** is the largest agency in the state and serves as the central administrative body for most health, welfare, and social services. It is the primary point of contact for Medicaid, licensing, and adult protection.

### A. DHHS Customer Service and General Inquiries

The DHHS Customer Service Center acts as the gateway for general inquiries, application assistance, and initial reporting for various programs.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DHHS Customer Service Center** | General inquiries, application assistance for Medicaid, SNAP, and cash assistance. | **(800) 852-3345** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/) [1] |
| **DHHS Mailing Address** | Official correspondence for the Department. | N/A | 129 Pleasant Street, Concord, NH 03301 |

### B. Medicaid Program Administration

The **New Hampshire Medicaid** program, officially Medical Assistance, is administered by the DHHS and is crucial for providing healthcare coverage and funding long-term services and supports, including the **Choices for Independence (CFI) Waiver**.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Hampshire Medicaid** | Information on eligibility, covered services, and managed care enrollment. | **(800) 852-3345** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/medicaid](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/medicaid) [2] |
| **Bureau of Adult and Aging Services (BAAS)** | Oversees the CFI Waiver and other long-term care services. | (603) 271-9203 | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/adult-aging-care](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/adult-aging-care) [3] |

The DHHS Customer Service line is the recommended first point of contact for all Medicaid-related questions, including the status of an application or questions about benefits.

## II. Licensing and Regulatory Oversight

The DHHS Health Facilities Administration is responsible for ensuring that all healthcare and residential facilities in New Hampshire meet state standards for safety and quality of care.

### A. Health Facilities Licensing Unit

This unit is responsible for the licensing, inspection, and investigation of complaints against facilities such as nursing homes, assisted living residences, and hospitals.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Health Facilities Licensing Unit** | Licensing and regulatory oversight; receiving and investigating complaints against licensed health facilities. | **(603) 271-9039** | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/health-facilities-licensing](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/health-facilities-licensing) [4] |
| **Complaint Email** | Submission of written complaints regarding facility standards. | N/A | `HFA-LIcensing@dhhs.nh.gov` [5] |

When contacting the Licensing Unit, it is essential to provide the full name and address of the facility, along with a detailed description of the alleged violation, including dates and times.

## III. Advocacy and Protection Services

New Hampshire provides dedicated resources for advocating for the rights of long-term care residents and for investigating cases of abuse, neglect, and exploitation of vulnerable adults.

### A. Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)

The **New Hampshire State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** is an independent advocate for residents in nursing homes and assisted living facilities. Their services are confidential and focused on resolving complaints related to resident rights and quality of life.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** | Advocacy for residents; confidential investigation and resolution of complaints. | **(800) 442-5640** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/long-term-care-ombudsman-program](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/programs-services/long-term-care-ombudsman-program) [6] |

The Ombudsman is the recommended first point of contact for residents or family members who have concerns about the care or treatment received within a long-term care facility.

### B. Adult Protective Services (APS)

APS is the state agency responsible for investigating reports of abuse, neglect, and financial exploitation of vulnerable adults who are unable to protect themselves.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adult Protection Reporting Line** | Reporting suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult. | **(800) 852-3345** (Toll-Free) | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/adult-protection-reporting-line](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/adult-protection-reporting-line) [7] |
| **APS Central Intake Email** | Submission of written reports of suspected abuse. | N/A | `APSCentralIntake@dhhs.nh.gov` |

**Note:** The APS reporting line is the same as the general DHHS Customer Service number, but callers should specify that they are reporting suspected adult abuse or neglect to be routed to the correct intake unit.

## IV. Veterans Services and Support

The **New Hampshire Office of Veterans Services (OVS)** is the state's official resource for connecting veterans and their families with state and federal benefits, including the State Veterans Home.

### A. Office of Veterans Services (OVS)

The OVS provides advocacy, information, and assistance with securing benefits such as property tax credits, educational assistance, and financial aid.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Hampshire Office of Veterans Services** | Advocacy and assistance with state and federal veterans benefits. | **(603) 624-9230** | [https://www.dmavs.nh.gov/veterans-services](https://www.dmavs.nh.gov/veterans-services) [8] |
| **Parent Department** | Department of Military Affairs and Veterans Services (DMAVS) | N/A | [https://www.nhveterans.nh.gov/](https://www.nhveterans.nh.gov/) |

The NHVH, located in Tilton, is the state's only long-term care facility dedicated to New Hampshire veterans, providing skilled nursing and residential care.

## V. Administrative Appeals and Fair Hearings

For individuals who disagree with a formal decision made by the DHHS regarding their eligibility for benefits or services, the **Administrative Appeals Unit (AAU)** provides an impartial review process.

### A. Administrative Appeals Unit (AAU)

The AAU conducts fair hearings for decisions related to Medicaid, SNAP, cash assistance, and other DHHS programs.

| Agency/Unit | Primary Role | Phone Number | Official Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Administrative Appeals Unit (AAU)** | Conducting impartial hearings for appeals of DHHS decisions. | **(603) 271-4292** | [https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/doing-business-dhhs/legal-services/administrative-appeals](https://www.dhhs.nh.gov/doing-business-dhhs/legal-services/administrative-appeals) [10] |
| **Mailing Address for Appeals** | Submission of the official Appeal Request Form. | N/A | 129 Pleasant Street, Concord, NH 03301 |

Appeals must be filed in writing and typically within a strict deadline from the date on the notice of decision.

## VI. Conclusion: Utilizing New Hampshire's Authority Contacts

The State of New Hampshire has established a clear and accessible network of authority contacts to manage its public services and regulatory functions. Understanding the specific mandate of each agency is the key to efficient problem resolution.

*   For **Medicaid eligibility** or general health service questions, contact the **DHHS Customer Service Center** at **(800) 852-3345**.
*   To **report poor care or facility standards** in a nursing home or assisted living residence, contact the **Health Facilities Licensing Unit** at **(603) 271-9039**.
*   For **resident rights advocacy** and confidential complaint resolution, contact the **Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** at **(800) 442-5640**.
*   To **report suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation**, use the **Adult Protection Reporting Line** at **(800) 852-3345**.
*   For **veterans benefits** and assistance, contact the **Office of Veterans Services** at **(603) 624-9230**.

By utilizing this verified directory, New Hampshire residents and their families can effectively engage with the appropriate state authorities to ensure their rights are protected, their benefits are secured, and their quality of care is maintained.
