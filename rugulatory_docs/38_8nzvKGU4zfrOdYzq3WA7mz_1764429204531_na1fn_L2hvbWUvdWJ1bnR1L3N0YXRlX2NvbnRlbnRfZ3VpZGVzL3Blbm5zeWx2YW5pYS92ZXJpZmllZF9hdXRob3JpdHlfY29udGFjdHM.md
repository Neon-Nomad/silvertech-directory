# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care and Veterans Benefits in Pennsylvania

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, healthcare benefits, and veterans' services in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania requires direct access to the correct state agencies and official resources. This comprehensive guide consolidates the verified contact information for the key governmental and advocacy organizations responsible for licensing facilities, administering Medicaid, protecting resident rights, and providing veterans' benefits.

This document is designed to serve as a single, authoritative reference point, detailing the specific role of each agency and the most effective way to reach them for information, assistance, or to file a complaint.

## I. Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS)

The **Department of Human Services (DHS)** is the largest state agency in Pennsylvania and serves as the state's Medicaid agency, officially known as **Medical Assistance (MA)**. DHS is also responsible for the licensing and oversight of residential care facilities, including Assisted Living Residences (ALRs) and Personal Care Homes (PCHs).

### A. Medicaid (Medical Assistance) and Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS)

DHS administers the Medical Assistance program, including the **Community HealthChoices (CHC) Waiver**, which is the primary funding source for long-term care services in the community, such as those provided in assisted living settings.

| Function | Contact Information | Details and Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Medicaid Information** | **1-866-550-4355** | Toll-free number for general inquiries about Medical Assistance (Medicaid) eligibility, benefits, and coverage. |
| **Official Medicaid Website** | [https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dhs/resources/medicaid] [1] | The central online resource for all Medical Assistance programs, including eligibility rules and program updates. |
| **Online Application Portal** | **COMPASS** (Online System) | The official online portal for applying for Medical Assistance, SNAP, and other state benefits. Access is available through the DHS website. |
| **Community HealthChoices (CHC)** | **1-833-735-4416** (CHC Enrollment Helpline) | Contact for questions regarding enrollment in the CHC managed care program, which coordinates physical health and long-term services for eligible individuals. |

### B. Licensing and Complaint Reporting for Residential Care

The **Bureau of Human Services Licensing (BHSL)**, a division of DHS's Office of Long-Term Living (OLTL), is the licensing authority for Personal Care Homes and Assisted Living Residences.

| Function | Contact Information | Details and Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PCH/ALR Complaint Hotline** | **1-877-401-8835** | **Dedicated, toll-free hotline** for reporting complaints or concerns regarding the care, safety, or operations of a licensed **Personal Care Home (PCH)** or **Assisted Living Residence (ALR)**. |
| **BHSL Licensing Website** | [https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dhs/resources/licensing/pch-alr-licensing] [2] | Official resource for regulations, licensing requirements, and access to the online complaint form for PCHs and ALRs. |
| **Online Complaint Form** | [DHS BHSL Online Complaint Form] [3] | Direct link to the online portal for submitting a formal, written complaint against a PCH or ALR. |

## II. Pennsylvania Department of Health (DOH)

The **Department of Health (DOH)** is the state agency responsible for the licensing, inspection, and regulation of **Nursing Homes** (Skilled Nursing Facilities) and other in-patient healthcare facilities.

### A. Nursing Home Licensing and Complaint Reporting

The DOH's **Division of Nursing Care Facilities** enforces state and federal regulations to ensure quality of care in nursing homes.

| Function | Contact Information | Details and Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nursing Home Complaint Hotline** | **1-800-254-5164** | **Dedicated, toll-free hotline** for reporting complaints or concerns regarding the care, treatment, or rights of residents in a licensed **Nursing Home**. |
| **DOH Complaint Email** | `c-ncomplai@pa.gov` | Email address for submitting written complaints to the Division of Nursing Care Facilities. |
| **DOH Licensing Website** | [https://www.pa.gov/agencies/health/facilities/in-patient-healthcare-facilities/nursing-homes/patients-and-families] [4] | Provides information for patients and families, including guidance on choosing a nursing home and links to public survey reports. |
| **Online Complaint Form** | [DOH Online Complaint Form] [5] | Direct link to the DOH's online portal for submitting a formal, written complaint against a healthcare facility, including nursing homes. |

## III. Pennsylvania Department of Aging (PDA)

The **Department of Aging** is the state agency dedicated to promoting the independence and well-being of older adults. It houses the critical **Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** and the **Older Adult Protective Services (OAPS)** program.

### A. Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

The Ombudsman program is an advocacy service that works to resolve complaints on behalf of residents in nursing homes, assisted living residences, and personal care homes.

| Function | Contact Information | Details and Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ombudsman State Office** | **717-783-8975** | The main phone number for the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Office. This office can connect callers with the local Ombudsman representative in their county. |
| **Ombudsman Email** | `LTC-Ombudsman@pa.gov` | Email contact for the State Ombudsman Office for non-emergency inquiries and general program information. |
| **Ombudsman Website** | [https://www.pa.gov/services/aging/request-assistance-from-a-long-term-care-ombudsman] [6] | Official resource detailing the Ombudsman's role, the types of complaints they handle, and how to request assistance. |

### B. Older Adult Protective Services (OAPS)

OAPS is responsible for investigating reports of abuse, neglect, exploitation, and abandonment of adults aged 60 and older.

| Function | Contact Information | Details and Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statewide Elder Abuse Hotline (24/7)** | **1-800-490-8505** | **CRITICAL 24-HOUR, TOLL-FREE HOTLINE** for reporting suspected abuse, neglect, self-neglect, or financial exploitation of an older adult. This is the primary contact for all protective services reports. |
| **OAPS Website** | [https://www.pa.gov/agencies/aging/report-elder-abuse] [7] | Official guidance on recognizing and reporting elder abuse in Pennsylvania. |

## IV. Pennsylvania Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA)

The **Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA)** is the state's primary advocate for veterans, administering state-specific benefits and operating the State Veterans Homes.

### A. Veterans Benefits and Assistance

The DMVA provides information and assistance regarding state benefits, including financial aid, tax exemptions, and educational programs.

| Function | Contact Information | Details and Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **General Veterans Information** | **1-800-547-2838** | Toll-free number for general inquiries about state veterans benefits, programs, and services. |
| **DMVA Official Website** | [https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dmva] [8] | The central online hub for all DMVA programs, including financial assistance, State Veterans Homes, and educational benefits. |
| **County Veterans Affairs Offices** | **Local County VA Office** | Veterans are strongly encouraged to contact their local County Director of Veterans Affairs for personalized assistance with state and federal claims, paperwork, and benefits applications. |

## V. Summary of Critical Hotlines

For quick reference, the following table summarizes the most critical, direct-access hotlines for long-term care and protective services in Pennsylvania.

| Hotline Purpose | Administering Agency | Toll-Free Phone Number |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nursing Home Complaints** | Department of Health (DOH) | **1-800-254-5164** |
| **PCH/ALR Complaints** | DHS Bureau of Human Services Licensing (BHSL) | **1-877-401-8835** |
| **Older Adult Protective Services (Abuse/Neglect)** | Department of Aging (OAPS) | **1-800-490-8505** (24/7) |
| **General Veterans Benefits** | Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMVA) | **1-800-547-2838** |
| **Medicaid/Medical Assistance** | Department of Human Services (DHS) | **1-866-550-4355** |

### Importance of Using the Correct Contact

It is essential to use the correct contact for the specific facility type:

*   **DOH (1-800-254-5164):** Only for **Nursing Homes**.
*   **DHS BHSL (1-877-401-8835):** Only for **Personal Care Homes** and **Assisted Living Residences**.
*   **OAPS (1-800-490-8505):** Only for reports of **Abuse, Neglect, or Exploitation**.

Using the correct hotline ensures that the complaint is routed immediately to the appropriate regulatory body for timely investigation and action.
