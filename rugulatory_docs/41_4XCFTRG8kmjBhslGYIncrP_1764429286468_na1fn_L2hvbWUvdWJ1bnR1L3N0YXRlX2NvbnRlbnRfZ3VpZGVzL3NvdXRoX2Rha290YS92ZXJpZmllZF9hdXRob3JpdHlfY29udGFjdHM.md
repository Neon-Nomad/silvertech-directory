This comprehensive guide consolidates the verified contact information for the primary state authorities responsible for long-term care in South Dakota. It details the specific functions of each agency, ensuring residents, family members, and caregivers can quickly access the resources they need to make informed decisions and secure quality care.

## 1. South Dakota Department of Social Services (DSS) - Medicaid

The **South Dakota Department of Social Services (DSS)** is the state agency responsible for administering the **South Dakota Medicaid** program. This program is the primary source of financial assistance for long-term care services, including nursing home care and community-based alternatives.

### Key Functions of the DSS Medicaid Division

The DSS is the authority for all matters related to Medicaid eligibility, enrollment, and policy. Its key functions include:

*   **Eligibility Determination:** Processing applications and determining financial and medical eligibility for all Medicaid programs, including the Aged, Blind, and Disabled (ABD) pathway for long-term care.
*   **HOPE Waiver Administration:** Overseeing the financial eligibility component of the **Home and Community-Based Options and Person-Centered Excellence (HOPE) Waiver**, which funds assisted living and in-home care services.
*   **Policy and Rules:** Establishing and enforcing the financial rules for long-term care Medicaid, including the income and asset limits and the Medicaid Look-Back Period.

## 2. South Dakota Department of Health (DOH) - Licensing and Complaints

The **South Dakota Department of Health (DOH)**, through its **Office of Licensure and Certification (OLC)**, is the regulatory body for all licensed healthcare facilities in the state. This agency is the primary authority for ensuring that facilities meet minimum standards of health, safety, and quality of care.

### Key Functions of the OLC

The OLC serves a dual role: licensing facilities and investigating serious complaints.

#### A. Licensing Authority

The OLC is responsible for the initial licensing and annual renewal of long-term care facilities, including:

*   **Nursing Facilities:** Licensing and certifying facilities that provide skilled nursing care.
*   **Assisted Living Centers:** Licensing facilities that provide supportive services and personal care.
*   **Public Records:** Maintaining and providing access to public records, including facility licensing status and survey reports.

#### B. Health Facility Complaint Process

The OLC operates the official complaint system for allegations of abuse, neglect, and poor quality of care in licensed facilities. This is the authority that conducts on-site investigations and imposes enforcement actions.

*   **Complaint Hotline:** The OLC's general phone number serves as the primary complaint hotline.
*   **Confidentiality:** The OLC maintains the confidentiality of the complainant's identity during the investigation process.

## 3. South Dakota Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

The **South Dakota Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)** is an independent advocacy program housed within the Department of Human Services (DHS). Its mission is to protect the rights, health, safety, and welfare of residents in long-term care facilities.

### Key Functions of the LTCOP

The Ombudsman is the resident's voice and is the ideal first point of contact for resolving quality of life and rights-related issues.

*   **Complaint Resolution:** Investigating and resolving complaints made by or on behalf of residents in nursing homes, assisted living centers, and other long-term care settings.
*   **Resident Rights Education:** Informing residents, families, and facility staff about resident rights under state and federal law.
*   **Advocacy:** Working to ensure that residents are treated with dignity and respect and that their preferences are honored.

## 4. South Dakota Department of Veterans Affairs (SDDVA)

The **South Dakota Department of Veterans Affairs (SDDVA)** is the state agency dedicated to assisting veterans and their families in accessing state and federal benefits, including long-term care options.

### Key Functions of the SDDVA

The SDDVA is the authority for state-level veterans' benefits and the operator of the state's veterans' home.

*   **State Veterans Home:** Overseeing the operation and admission process for the **Michael J. Fitzmaurice State Veterans Home** in Hot Springs, which provides skilled nursing care.
*   **Benefit Assistance:** Assisting veterans with claims for federal benefits, such as VA Disability Compensation and the Aid and Attendance Pension, which can fund long-term care.
*   **State Benefits:** Administering state-specific benefits, including property tax exemptions for disabled veterans.

## 5. Additional Critical Advocacy and Reporting Contacts

Beyond the primary long-term care authorities, several other agencies play a vital role in protecting vulnerable adults in South Dakota.

For immediate concerns regarding the abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult, regardless of their living situation, the matter should be reported to Adult Protective Services.
