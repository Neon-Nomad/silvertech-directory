# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Virginia

Navigating the long-term care system in Virginia requires connecting with several key state agencies responsible for Medicaid, licensing, advocacy, and veterans' benefits. This document provides a consolidated and verified list of essential contact information for the primary governmental and non-profit authorities that oversee and support long-term care services in the Commonwealth.

## I. Medicaid and Long-Term Services and Supports (LTSS)

The **Department of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS)** administers Virginia's Medicaid program, known as **Cardinal Care**, and manages the state's Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers, including the critical Commonwealth Coordinated Care Plus (CCC Plus) Waiver.

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Department of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS)** | | State Medicaid Agency |
| Website | [https://www.dmas.virginia.gov/](https://www.dmas.virginia.gov/) [1] | Official DMAS website |
| General Phone | (855) 242-8282 | General inquiries and assistance |
| **CCC Plus Waiver Information** | | Commonwealth Coordinated Care Plus |
| Website | [https://www.dmas.virginia.gov/for-members/benefits-and-services/waivers/ccc-plus-waiver/](https://www.dmas.virginia.gov/for-members/benefits-and-services/waivers/ccc-plus-waiver/) [2] | Information on waiver services and eligibility |
| **CommonHelp** | [https://commonhelp.virginia.gov/](https://commonhelp.virginia.gov/) [3] | Online application for Medicaid and other benefits |

## II. Facility Licensing and Regulation

Licensing and regulatory oversight for long-term care facilities in Virginia are divided between the Department of Health (VDH) for medical facilities and the Department of Social Services (VDSS) for residential facilities.

### A. Nursing Homes and Medical Facilities (VDH)

The **Virginia Department of Health (VDH), Office of Licensure and Certification (OLC)**, is responsible for licensing and certifying nursing homes, hospitals, and other medical facilities.

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VDH Office of Licensure and Certification (OLC)** | | Nursing Home Licensing and Certification |
| Website | [https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/licensure-and-certification/](https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/licensure-and-certification/) [4] | Official OLC website |
| General Phone | (804) 367-2100 | General licensing and certification inquiries |
| Inspection Reports | [VDH OLC Inspections and Surveys](https://www.vdh.virginia.gov/licensure-and-certification/division-of-long-term-care-services/ltc-inspections-surveys/) [5] | Public access to nursing home inspection reports |

### B. Assisted Living Facilities (VDSS)

The **Virginia Department of Social Services (VDSS)**, through its Division of Licensing Programs, is responsible for licensing and regulating Assisted Living Facilities (ALFs).

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VDSS Division of Licensing Programs** | | Assisted Living Facility Licensing |
| Website | [https://www.dss.virginia.gov/facility/alf.cgi](https://www.dss.virginia.gov/facility/alf.cgi) [6] | ALF licensing information and resources |
| Facility Search | [VDSS Search for an Assisted Living Facility](https://www.dss.virginia.gov/facility/search/alf.cgi) [7] | Search for licensed facilities and view inspection summaries |

## III. Complaint Reporting and Resident Advocacy

Virginia provides multiple channels for reporting concerns about care quality, abuse, and resident rights.

### A. Official Complaint Hotlines

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VDH OLC Complaint Hotline** | **(800) 955-1819** [8] | **To report complaints against Nursing Homes, Hospitals, and Home Health Agencies.** |
| **Adult Protective Services (APS) Hotline** | **(888) 832-3858** [9] | **To report suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of adults (age 60+) or incapacitated adults.** |

### B. Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program

The **Virginia Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)** provides confidential advocacy for residents in nursing homes and assisted living facilities.

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** | | Resident Rights and Complaint Resolution |
| Website | [https://www.elderrights.virginia.gov/](https://www.elderrights.virginia.gov/) [10] | Official LTCOP website |
| Toll-Free Phone | (800) 552-5019 | To contact a local Ombudsman |

### C. Legal and Disability Advocacy

| Agency/Program | Contact Detail | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statewide Senior Legal Helpline** | (844) 802-5910 [11] | Free legal advice for Virginians aged 60+ on issues like Medicaid and long-term care. |
| **disAbility Law Center of Virginia (dLCV)** | (800) 552-3962 [12] | Protection and Advocacy for individuals with disabilities in long-term care settings. |
