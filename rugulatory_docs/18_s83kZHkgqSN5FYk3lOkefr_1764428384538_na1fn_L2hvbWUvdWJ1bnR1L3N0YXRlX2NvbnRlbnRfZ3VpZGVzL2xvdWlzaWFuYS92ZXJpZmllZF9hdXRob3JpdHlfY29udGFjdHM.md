# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in Louisiana

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, assisted living, and senior services in Louisiana requires access to accurate and verified contact information for the state’s key regulatory, advocacy, and benefit-providing agencies. This comprehensive guide consolidates the essential contact details for the Louisiana Department of Health (LDH), the Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program, the Department of Veterans Affairs (LDVA), and other critical authorities.

Having these verified contacts readily available is the first step for residents, family members, and caregivers seeking to apply for benefits, file a complaint, or advocate for the rights of a loved one in the Pelican State.

## 1. State Medicaid and Long-Term Care Services

The **Louisiana Department of Health (LDH)** is the central authority for Medicaid and the state’s primary long-term care programs. Understanding which division to contact is crucial for eligibility, enrollment, and service coordination.

### Louisiana Department of Health (LDH)

The LDH, through its **Bureau of Health Services Financing**, administers the Louisiana Medicaid program, known as **Healthy Louisiana**. This agency is responsible for setting policy, managing the Managed Care Organizations (MCOs), and overseeing the state’s Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers, such as the Community Choices Waiver (CCW) [1].

| Agency | Purpose | Contact Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Louisiana Department of Health (LDH)** | General Medicaid information, policy, and administration. | **Phone:** (888) 342-6207 (Toll-Free) |
| **LDH Website** | Official portal for all Medicaid programs and services. | **Web:** [https://ldh.la.gov/medicaid](https://ldh.la.gov/medicaid) |

### Louisiana Options in Long Term Care (OLTC)

For individuals seeking to access HCBS waivers, such as the Community Choices Waiver (CCW) which supports assisted living, the first point of contact is **Louisiana Options in Long Term Care (OLTC)**. This entity manages the Request for Services Registry (RFSR), which is the waiting list for waiver services [2].

*   **Role:** OLTC provides initial screening and places eligible individuals on the waiting list for long-term care services that help them remain in the community.
*   **Importance:** Contacting OLTC is mandatory to begin the process of applying for Medicaid-funded assisted living support through the CCW.

| Agency | Purpose | Contact Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Louisiana Options in Long Term Care (OLTC)** | Screening and registration for the Community Choices Waiver (CCW) and other long-term care services. | **Phone:** (877) 456-1146 (Toll-Free) |

## 2. Licensing, Regulation, and Facility Oversight

The **LDH Health Standards Section (HSS)** is the regulatory arm of the state, responsible for ensuring that all licensed healthcare facilities, including nursing homes and assisted living facilities, meet minimum standards of care and safety.

### LDH Health Standards Section (HSS)

HSS conducts licensing, certification, and unannounced inspections of facilities. Contacting HSS is appropriate for general inquiries about a facility's license status, regulatory compliance, or to request public records of inspection reports [3].

| Agency | Purpose | Contact Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LDH Health Standards Section (HSS)** | General licensing and regulatory inquiries. | **Phone:** (225) 342-0138 |
| **HSS Website** | Information on licensing, rulemaking, and facility types (Nursing Facilities, ARCPs). | **Web:** [https://ldh.la.gov/health-standards-section](https://ldh.la.gov/health-standards-section) |

### Public Records Request

To obtain copies of a facility's inspection reports (Statements of Deficiencies) or complaint investigation findings, a formal public records request must be submitted to the HSS. This process ensures transparency and allows consumers to review a facility's compliance history [4].

*   **Process:** The HSS maintains a specific procedure for submitting and processing public records requests, which may involve a fee for copying and processing.

## 3. Complaint Reporting and Adult Protection

Louisiana provides multiple dedicated channels for reporting concerns, ranging from quality of care issues to allegations of abuse, neglect, and exploitation.

### HSS Complaint Hotlines

The HSS operates specific hotlines for reporting complaints against licensed facilities. The severity of the issue determines the urgency of the HSS's response, with allegations of immediate jeopardy receiving the fastest on-site investigation [5].

| Facility Type | Complaint Focus | HSS Complaint Hotline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nursing Homes** | Abuse, neglect, and quality of care complaints. | **(888) 810-1819** (Toll-Free) |
| **Adult Residential Care Providers (ARCPs)** | Complaints against assisted living facilities. | **(225) 342-6298** |
| **All Other Licensed Facilities** | General complaints against other healthcare providers. | **(225) 342-0138** |

### Adult Protective Services (APS)

For allegations of abuse, neglect, self-neglect, or exploitation of vulnerable adults who live in the community (not just in licensed facilities), the **Adult Protective Services (APS)** program is the appropriate authority. APS investigates and intervenes to protect adults aged 18 and older who are unable to protect themselves [6].

| Agency | Purpose | Contact Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adult Protective Services (APS)** | Reporting abuse, neglect, or exploitation of vulnerable adults in the community. | **Phone:** (800) 898-4910 (Toll-Free) |

## 4. Advocacy and Resident Rights

For residents and families seeking mediation, advocacy, or assistance with asserting resident rights, the Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program is the primary resource.

### Louisiana Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)

The LTCOP, housed within the **Governor's Office of Elderly Affairs (GOEA)**, is an independent advocate for residents in nursing homes and assisted living facilities. Ombudsmen investigate and resolve complaints confidentially, focusing on the resident's wishes and rights [7].

*   **Role:** The Ombudsman is a resident advocate and mediator, not a regulator. They work to resolve issues such as dignity, respect, quality of life, and disputes with facility management.
*   **Confidentiality:** The Ombudsman must obtain the resident's consent before investigating a complaint or disclosing their identity, ensuring protection against retaliation.

| Agency | Purpose | Contact Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LTCOP (Governor's Office of Elderly Affairs)** | Advocacy, complaint resolution, and protection of resident rights in long-term care facilities. | **Phone:** (866) 632-0922 (Toll-Free) |
| **GOEA Website** | Information on the Ombudsman Program and other elderly affairs services. | **Web:** [https://goea.louisiana.gov/services/louisiana-ombudsman-program/](https://goea.louisiana.gov/services/louisiana-ombudsman-program/) |

### Disability Rights Louisiana (DRLA)

As the federally authorized Protection and Advocacy (P&A) system for Louisiana, DRLA provides legal and advocacy services to people with disabilities, including those in long-term care settings. They also manage the **Community Living Ombudsman Program (CLOP)**, which focuses on individuals with developmental disabilities in community settings [8].

| Agency | Purpose | Contact Information |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Disability Rights Louisiana (DRLA)** | Legal and systemic advocacy for people with disabilities. | **Phone:** (800) 960-7705 (Toll-Free) |
| **DRLA Website** | Resources and contact for the Community Living Ombudsman Program (CLOP). | **Web:** [https://disabilityrightsla.org/](https://disabilityrightsla.org/) |

## 5. Veterans Benefits and Long-Term Care

The **Louisiana Department of Veterans Affairs (LDVA)** is the state agency dedicated to assisting veterans, their spouses, and dependents in obtaining state and federal benefits, including long-term care options.

### Louisiana Department of Veterans Affairs (LDVA)

The LDVA operates the five State Veterans Homes and provides crucial assistance with filing claims for federal benefits like the VA Aid and Attendance Pension, which can help finance assisted living [9].
