# New Mexico Verified Authority Contacts: Essential Resources for Health, Licensing, and Advocacy

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, Medicaid, and veterans' services in New Mexico requires direct access to the correct state agencies and departments. This document provides a verified, comprehensive list of essential contact information for the key governmental and advocacy organizations responsible for oversight, benefits administration, and resident protection in the state.

This information is crucial for residents, family members, and caregivers seeking to apply for benefits, file a complaint, or access support services.

## I. Medicaid and Health Care Authority Contacts

The **New Mexico Health Care Authority (HCA)** is the state agency responsible for administering the Medicaid program, known as **Turquoise Care**, and overseeing health facility licensing and certification.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Mexico Health Care Authority (HCA)** | **Website:** [https://www.hca.nm.gov/]() | State agency administering Medicaid (Turquoise Care) and health facility licensing. |
| **Turquoise Care (Medicaid) Information** | **Phone:** **1-800-283-4465** [1] | General information, eligibility, and application assistance for the Turquoise Care Medicaid program. |
| **Medicaid Application Portal** | **Website:** [YES.NM.GOV]() | Online portal for applying for Medicaid and other state benefits. |
| **Health Facility Licensing & Certification** | **Agency:** HCA, Division of Health Improvement (DHI) | Responsible for licensing and certifying nursing homes, assisted living, and other health facilities. |
| **Health Facility Complaint Hotline** | **Phone:** **1-800-752-8649** [2] | To report complaints regarding quality of care, regulatory violations, and abuse/neglect in licensed health facilities. |
| **Health Facility Provider Search** | **Website:** [https://www.hca.nm.gov/health-facility-provider-search/]() | Public access to view inspection and survey reports for licensed health facilities. |

## II. Long-Term Care Ombudsman and Advocacy Contacts

The **New Mexico Aging and Long-Term Services Department (ALTSD)** is the primary agency for services and advocacy for seniors and adults with disabilities.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Mexico Aging and Long-Term Services Department (ALTSD)** | **Website:** [https://www.aging.nm.gov/]() | State agency providing services and advocacy for older adults and people with disabilities. |
| **Long-Term Care Ombudsman Program** | **Toll-Free Phone:** **1-866-451-2901** [3] | Independent advocates for residents in nursing homes and assisted living facilities; for resident rights and quality of life complaints. |
| **Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC)** | **Toll-Free Phone:** **1-800-432-2080** [4] | Single point of entry for information, referral, and assistance on all long-term care options and services. |
| **Adult Protective Services (APS)** | **Toll-Free Phone:** **1-800-432-2080** [5] | To report suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of vulnerable adults (same number as ADRC). |

## III. Veterans Benefits and Services Contacts

The **New Mexico Department of Veterans' Services (NMDVS)** is dedicated to assisting veterans and their families in obtaining state and federal benefits.

| Agency/Program | Contact Information | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New Mexico Department of Veterans' Services (NMDVS)** | **Website:** [https://www.nmdvs.org/]() | State agency assisting veterans with benefits, claims, and support services. |
| **NMDVS General Information** | **Phone:** **1-866-433-8387** [6] | General inquiries and assistance with state and federal veterans' benefits. |
| **New Mexico State Veterans' Home** | **Location:** Truth or Consequences, NM | Provides skilled nursing and long-term care for eligible veterans and their spouses. |
| **NMDVS Field Offices** | **Website:** [https://www.nmdvs.org/field-offices/]() | Locate a local Veteran Service Officer (VSO) for claims assistance and counseling. |

## IV. Key Findings and Program Summary

The following table summarizes the key long-term care programs and their administering authorities in New Mexico.

| Program/Authority | Administering Agency | Key Function | Primary Contact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Medicaid Program Name** | New Mexico Health Care Authority (HCA) | Provides comprehensive health coverage and long-term services. | **1-800-283-4465** |
