# New Jersey Veterans Benefits: Comprehensive Guide to Long-Term Care and Support

The state of New Jersey, through the **Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMAVA)**, is committed to honoring its veterans by providing a wide array of benefits, with a significant focus on long-term care and support services. This comprehensive guide details the state-operated Veterans Memorial Homes, the critical federal benefits like Aid and Attendance, and other state-specific programs designed to ensure the health, dignity, and well-being of New Jersey’s military community.

## The New Jersey Veterans Memorial Homes

New Jersey operates three state-of-the-art long-term care nursing homes, known as the **New Jersey Veterans Memorial Homes**. These facilities are dedicated to providing the highest level of skilled nursing care in an environment that respects the service and sacrifice of its residents.

### Locations and Administration

The three Veterans Memorial Homes are strategically located across the state to serve veterans in different regions:

The homes are administered by the Division of Veterans Healthcare Services, a component of the New Jersey Department of Military and Veterans Affairs (DMAVA) [1].

### Services and Amenities

The Veterans Memorial Homes are designed to provide comprehensive, person-centered care that goes beyond the standard nursing home model. They offer a full spectrum of services to meet the medical, physical, social, and emotional needs of their residents:

*   **Skilled Nursing Care:** 24/7 medical and nursing care provided by a full-time staff of physicians, nurse practitioners, registered nurses, and licensed practical nurses [2].
*   **Rehabilitation Services:** Physical, occupational, and speech therapy services are provided to maximize residents' independence and quality of life.
*   **Specialized Care:** Services include hospice care, medication management, memory care for residents with Alzheimer's or other dementias, and specialized dietary services [3].
*   **Residential Options:** The Vineland home, for example, offers a unique feature of 28 apartment-style rooms, allowing residents to maintain a higher level of independence while still having access to skilled care [4].
*   **Amenities and Activities:** The facilities feature a variety of amenities to promote socialization and comfort, such as a "Town Square" core, fully equipped beauty and barber shops, lounges, and organized recreational and social programs [5].

### Eligibility and Admission Requirements

Admission to a New Jersey Veterans Memorial Home is based on both military service and medical/financial need.

#### Military Service Requirements

To be eligible for admission, an applicant must be a veteran who meets the following criteria [6]:

*   **Service:** Must have served on active duty in the Armed Forces of the United States during a period of war or other emergency as proclaimed by the Governor or the President of the United States.
*   **Discharge:** Must have been discharged or released from service under conditions other than dishonorable.
*   **Spouses and Gold Star Parents:** In certain circumstances, the spouse of an eligible veteran, or a Gold Star Parent (a parent of a service member who died while serving), may also be eligible for admission, provided the home has capacity [7].

#### Residency and Medical Requirements

*   **Residency:** Preference for admission is given to veteran applicants who have been residents of New Jersey for at least two years immediately prior to the date of application [8].
*   **Medical Need:** Applicants must have a qualified medical need for skilled nursing care or long-term care services, as determined by the home's admissions committee [9].

### Cost of Care and Financial Considerations

While the homes are state-operated, residents are generally required to contribute to the cost of their care based on their ability to pay.

*   **Resident Fee:** The resident's fee is calculated as **80% of their net income**, but this fee cannot exceed the "Established Rate" determined annually by the Adjutant General [10].
*   **Federal Subsidy:** The state receives a federal per diem subsidy from the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) for each eligible veteran resident, which significantly offsets the cost of care and helps keep the resident's portion affordable [11].
*   **Medicaid and Medicare:** The Veterans Memorial Homes are certified to accept Medicare and Medicaid (NJ FamilyCare) benefits, which can be used to cover the cost of care for eligible residents.

## Federal Long-Term Care Benefits for New Jersey Veterans

In addition to the state-run homes, New Jersey veterans can access crucial federal benefits that provide financial assistance for long-term care in a variety of settings, including their own homes, assisted living facilities, and private nursing homes.

### 1. VA Aid and Attendance (A&A) Pension

The **Aid and Attendance (A&A) Pension** is a tax-free monetary benefit paid in addition to the basic VA pension. It is designed for wartime veterans and their surviving spouses who require the aid of another person to perform daily functions or are housebound.

#### Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for the A&A benefit, a veteran must meet the basic VA pension requirements (service during a wartime period, limited income, and net worth) **and** one of the following medical criteria [12]:

*   Requires assistance with activities of daily living (ADLs) such as bathing, feeding, dressing, or toileting.
*   Is confined to bed due to a disability.
*   Is a patient in a nursing home due to mental or physical incapacity.
*   Has severely impaired eyesight (5/200 visual acuity or less).

#### Benefit Usage

The A&A benefit is a vital resource for New Jersey veterans, as it can be used to pay for:

*   **In-Home Care:** Services provided by a home health aide or personal care attendant.
*   **Assisted Living:** The monthly cost of residency and care services in a licensed assisted living facility.
*   **Nursing Home Care:** The cost of care in a private nursing home.

#### The Application Process

The application for the A&A benefit is known to be complex and requires meticulous documentation.

1.  **Gather Documentation:** Required documents include the veteran's discharge papers (DD-214), medical evidence (VA Form 21-2680, Examination for Housebound Status or Permanent Need for Regular Aid and Attendance), and financial information [13].
2.  **Submit Application:** The application can be submitted to the VA Regional Office. Due to the complexity, many New Jersey veterans choose to work with an accredited Veterans Service Officer (VSO) or an elder law attorney specializing in VA benefits to ensure the application is completed correctly and to maximize the chances of approval [14].

### 2. VA Health Care System Long-Term Care

The **VA New Jersey Health Care System** provides a range of long-term care services for enrolled veterans, including:

*   **Home-Based Primary Care (HBPC):** Comprehensive primary care services delivered to veterans in their homes.
*   **Skilled Home Health Care:** Short-term, skilled services like nursing care and physical therapy at home.
*   **Community Living Centers (VA Nursing Homes):** The VA operates its own nursing homes, separate from the state-run Veterans Memorial Homes.
*   **Respite Care:** Services to provide relief for a veteran's primary caregiver [15].

## New Jersey State-Specific Financial and Support Benefits

Beyond the Veterans Memorial Homes, the DMAVA offers several other state-specific benefits that can impact a veteran's financial stability and access to care.

### 1. Property Tax Exemptions

New Jersey offers a significant property tax benefit to veterans:

*   **Full Exemption:** A veteran who is certified by the VA as having a **100% permanent and total service-connected disability** is entitled to a full exemption from property taxes on their primary residence [16]. This can provide substantial financial relief, freeing up resources for long-term care needs.
*   **Annual Deduction:** Veterans who served during a period of war or conflict are eligible for an annual **\$250 property tax deduction** on their primary residence [16].

### 2. Income Tax Exemption

New Jersey veterans are eligible for a **\$6,000 exemption** on their New Jersey gross income tax return, provided they are honorably discharged and served on active duty [17]. This tax relief can contribute to a veteran's overall financial resources for care.

### 3. Support and Referral Services

The DMAVA's **Veterans Benefits Bureau** provides direct assistance and referral services to veterans and their families, including:

*   **Homeless Veteran Care:** DMAVA offers referral assistance to veterans who are homeless, involuntarily displaced, or in imminent danger of losing their home, connecting them with housing and support resources [18].
*   **Veterans Service Officers (VSOs):** The DMAVA employs VSOs who are trained to assist veterans in applying for all federal and state benefits, including Aid and Attendance and disability compensation. These services are provided free of charge [19].

## Conclusion

New Jersey stands as a strong advocate for its veteran population, offering a comprehensive suite of long-term care options. The three state-run Veterans Memorial Homes provide high-quality, dedicated skilled nursing care, while the DMAVA actively connects veterans to vital financial resources like the property tax exemption and the federal Aid and Attendance pension. For any veteran or surviving spouse in New Jersey seeking long-term care, the first step should be to contact the DMAVA or an accredited Veterans Service Officer to ensure all available state and federal benefits are fully utilized.
