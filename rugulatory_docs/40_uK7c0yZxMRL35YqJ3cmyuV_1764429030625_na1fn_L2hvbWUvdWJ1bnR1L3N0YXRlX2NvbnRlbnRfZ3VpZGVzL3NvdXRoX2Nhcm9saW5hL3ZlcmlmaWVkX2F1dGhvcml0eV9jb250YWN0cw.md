# Verified Authority Contacts for Long-Term Care in South Carolina: A Comprehensive Directory

Navigating the complex landscape of long-term care, healthcare benefits, and regulatory oversight in **South Carolina** requires access to accurate and reliable information. This guide serves as a comprehensive directory of **Verified Authority Contacts**, providing the essential phone numbers, websites, and detailed descriptions of the state agencies responsible for Medicaid, facility licensing, resident advocacy, and veterans' benefits. These authorities are the official sources for information, applications, complaints, and protective services for vulnerable adults in the state.

The state's long-term care system is primarily overseen by three major governmental branches: the **South Carolina Department of Health and Human Services (SCDHHS)** for Medicaid, the **South Carolina Department of Public Health (DPH)** for licensing and regulation, and the **South Carolina Department on Aging (SCDOA)** and **Department of Social Services (DSS)** for advocacy and protection.

## I. Medicaid and Health Benefits Authority: SCDHHS

The **South Carolina Department of Health and Human Services (SCDHHS)** is the single state agency responsible for administering the state's Medicaid program, known as **Healthy Connections Medicaid**. This agency is the definitive source for information regarding eligibility, enrollment, covered services, and the state's Home and Community-Based Services (HCBS) waivers, such as the **Community Choices (CC) Waiver**.

### A. SCDHHS General and Program Contacts

The SCDHHS maintains several key contact points to assist residents, applicants, and providers with various aspects of the Medicaid program.

| SCDHHS Contact Point | Phone Number | Official Website/Resource | Primary Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Healthy Connections Medicaid General Inquiries** | **(888) 549-0820** | [https://www.scdhhs.gov/](https://www.scdhhs.gov/) | General eligibility, enrollment, and benefits questions for Healthy Connections Medicaid. |
| **Community Long-Term Care (CLTC) Referral Line** | **(888) 971-1637** | [https://www.scdhhs.gov/](https://www.scdhhs.gov/) | To initiate a referral or application for long-term care services, including the Community Choices Waiver. |
| **SCDHHS Official Website** | N/A | [https://www.scdhhs.gov/](https://www.scdhhs.gov/) | Online application, program manuals, policy updates, and member resources. |

### B. Detailed Function of SCDHHS Contacts

#### 1. Healthy Connections Medicaid General Inquiries
This toll-free number is the starting point for most inquiries about South Carolina's Medicaid program. Callers can receive assistance with:
*   Checking the status of a Medicaid application.
*   Understanding the different Medicaid coverage groups (e.g., Aged, Blind, or Disabled).
*   Questions about managed care plans (Healthy Connections Choices).
*   Locating a Medicaid provider or understanding covered services.

#### 2. Community Long-Term Care (CLTC) Referral Line
The CLTC division of SCDHHS manages the assessment and coordination of long-term care services. This line is crucial for individuals seeking alternatives to nursing home care. The CLTC team conducts the functional assessment to determine if an applicant meets the **Nursing Facility Level of Care (NFLOC)**, which is required for both institutional care and the Community Choices Waiver.

*   **Community Choices (CC) Waiver:** This waiver provides services like personal care, adult day health care, and respite care in a home or community setting, including Community Residential Care Facilities (CRCFs). The CLTC referral line is the gateway to accessing this vital program.

## II. Licensing and Regulatory Authority: SC Department of Public Health (DPH)

The **South Carolina Department of Public Health (DPH)**, specifically its **Bureau of Healthcare Quality**, is the state's regulatory body for healthcare facilities. This agency is responsible for ensuring that all licensed facilities, including nursing homes and CRCFs, comply with state licensing regulations and federal certification standards.

### A. DPH Regulatory and Complaint Contacts

The DPH provides direct channels for the public to access information on licensed facilities and to report concerns about the quality of care or safety.

| DPH Contact Point | Phone Number | Official Website/Resource | Primary Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DPH Healthcare Quality General Office** | **(803) 545-4370** | [https://dph.sc.gov/professionals/healthcare-quality/healthcare-facility-licensing](https://dph.sc.gov/professionals/healthcare-quality/healthcare-facility-licensing) | General inquiries about facility licensing, certification, and regulatory compliance. |
| **DPH Complaint Hotline** | **1-800-922-6735** | [https://dph.sc.gov/professionals/healthcare-quality/file-complaint](https://dph.sc.gov/professionals/healthcare-quality/file-complaint) | To report complaints against licensed healthcare facilities (nursing homes, hospitals, CRCFs) regarding quality of care, safety, or regulatory violations. |
| **DPH Official Website** | N/A | [https://dph.sc.gov/](https://dph.sc.gov/) | Access to facility inspection reports, licensing forms, and regulatory manuals. |

### B. Detailed Function of DPH Contacts

#### 1. DPH Healthcare Quality General Office
This office manages the administrative and regulatory aspects of facility oversight. It is the contact point for:
*   Verifying the licensed status of a nursing home or CRCF.
*   Questions about state regulations (e.g., Regulation 61-17 for Nursing Homes).
*   Information on the DHEC restructuring that led to the formation of the DPH and its new role.

#### 2. DPH Complaint Hotline (1-800-922-6735)
This toll-free number is the official intake for all facility-related complaints. The DPH is mandated to investigate all credible complaints regarding violations of state and federal standards. Complaints can be filed anonymously, and the DPH prioritizes investigations based on the severity of the alleged violation, with immediate jeopardy complaints being investigated within two working days. This hotline is critical for reporting issues such as:
*   Inadequate staffing or care.
*   Unsanitary conditions.
*   Medication errors.
*   Violations of resident rights that do not involve abuse.

## III. Advocacy and Protective Services Authorities

South Carolina has two primary agencies dedicated to the protection and advocacy of vulnerable adults: the Long Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP) for resident rights advocacy and Adult Protective Services (APS) for investigating abuse, neglect, and exploitation.

### A. Advocacy and Protection Contacts

These contacts provide non-regulatory support and protective services, offering a confidential channel for residents and families to voice concerns.

| Advocacy/Protection Authority | Phone Number | Official Website/Resource | Primary Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)** | **1-800-868-9095** | [https://aging.sc.gov/programs-initiatives/long-term-care-ombudsman-program](https://aging.sc.gov/programs-initiatives/long-term-care-ombudsman-program) | Confidential advocacy, mediation, and complaint resolution for residents of nursing homes and CRCFs. |
| **DSS Adult Protective Services (APS) Hotline** | **1-888-CARE4US (1-888-227-3487)** | [https://dss.sc.gov/adult-protection/](https://dss.sc.gov/adult-protection/) | To report suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation of a vulnerable adult (age 18+). |

### B. Detailed Function of Advocacy and Protection Contacts

#### 1. Long Term Care Ombudsman Program (LTCOP)
Administered by the **South Carolina Department on Aging (SCDOA)**, the LTCOP is an independent advocate for residents. The Ombudsman's role is to resolve complaints through mediation and negotiation, focusing on the resident's wishes and rights. The program is confidential and can assist with issues such as:
*   Quality of life concerns.
*   Disputes over transfer or discharge notices.
*   Violations of resident dignity and respect.
*   Educating residents and families on their rights.

#### 2. DSS Adult Protective Services (APS) Hotline
The DSS APS is the state's investigative authority for allegations of mistreatment of vulnerable adults. This 24/7 hotline is the mandated reporting channel for suspected:
*   **Abuse:** Physical, sexual, or emotional harm.
*   **Neglect:** Failure of a caregiver to provide necessary care, or self-neglect.
*   **Exploitation:** Illegal or improper use of a vulnerable adult's resources.

If the vulnerable adult is in immediate danger, **911** should be called first. For non-emergency reports of suspected mistreatment, the APS hotline is the appropriate contact for initiating a formal investigation.

## IV. Veterans' Benefits Authority: SCDVA

For veterans residing in South Carolina, the **South Carolina Department of Veterans' Affairs (SCDVA)** is the key state agency providing support, claims assistance, and access to state-specific benefits, including the State Veterans Homes.

### A. SCDVA Contacts

The SCDVA is dedicated to ensuring veterans and their families receive the benefits they have earned through their service.

| SCDVA Contact Point | Phone Number | Official Website/Resource | Primary Function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SCDVA General Office** | **(803) 734-0200** | [https://scdva.sc.gov/](https://scdva.sc.gov/) | General inquiries about state veterans benefits, programs, and services. |
| **SCDVA State Veterans Homes** | N/A (Contact through CVAOs) | [https://scdva.sc.gov/state-veterans-homes](https://scdva.sc.gov/state-veterans-homes) | Information on the six state-run veterans homes for skilled nursing care. |
| **County Veterans Affairs Officers (CVAOs)** | Varies by County | [https://scdva.sc.gov/](https://scdva.sc.gov/) | Direct assistance with filing federal VA claims for disability, pension, and long-term care. |

### B. Detailed Function of SCDVA Contacts

#### 1. SCDVA General Office
The SCDVA serves as the central hub for all veterans' affairs. They can provide information on:
*   State tax exemptions for military retirement and disability pay.
*   Educational benefits for veterans' dependents.
*   Employment preference programs.

#### 2. State Veterans Homes
South Carolina operates six State Veterans Homes, which provide subsidized skilled nursing care. While the SCDVA website provides general information, the application process for admission is typically coordinated through the local **County Veterans Affairs Officers (CVAOs)**.

#### 3. County Veterans Affairs Officers (CVAOs)
CVAOs are accredited representatives who provide free, expert assistance to veterans in filing claims with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). They are the most important contact for veterans seeking federal long-term care benefits, such as the **Aid and Attendance Pension** or admission to a VA Community Living Center.

## V. Importance of Utilizing Verified Authority Contacts

Relying on official state and federal authorities is paramount when dealing with healthcare, financial, and legal matters related to long-term care. These agencies provide:

*   **Accuracy and Reliability:** The information provided is based on current state and federal law and policy.
*   **Confidentiality and Protection:** Agencies like the LTCOP and DSS APS are legally mandated to protect the confidentiality of complainants and the safety of vulnerable adults.
*   **Legal Authority:** Only the DPH has the authority to inspect facilities and enforce regulatory compliance, and only the SCDHHS can determine Medicaid eligibility.

By using this verified directory, residents, family members, and caregivers in South Carolina can efficiently access the correct governmental resource to secure benefits, resolve complaints, and ensure the highest standard of care and dignity for their loved ones.
